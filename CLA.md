## How to sign

In your first pull request to Foedrum, add one line to [`.github/cla-signatures.md`](.github/cla-signatures.md), in a commit of its own:

```
- @<your GitHub login>, CLA 1.0, <today's date as YYYY-MM-DD>
```

By committing that line under your GitHub account you accept this Agreement, version 1.0, for all your Contributions, present and future. The repository's history records who signed which version and when. A check on every pull request fails until its author has signed.

## Changes from the Apache ICLA V2.2

- "the Foundation" is replaced by "the Maintainer", defined in section 1, and the Work is named as Foedrum.
- The form fields, signing by email and the privacy notice are replaced by "How to sign": a line in the signatures file, committed under the contributor's GitHub account.
- The Foundation's promise not to use Contributions contrary to the public benefit or its nonprofit status and bylaws is removed, since it belongs to the Foundation's charter.
- "submitted" names the Work's repository and issue tracker instead of the Foundation's mailing lists and systems.
- Section 2 adds the sentence on licensing under any terms, including licenses other than the GNU Affero General Public License and commercial licenses.
- Section 4 drops the option of a Corporate CLA, which Foedrum does not have.
- The agreement has a version, 1.0.
