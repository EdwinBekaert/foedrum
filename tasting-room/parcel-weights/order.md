Read a parcel's weight

The counter asks for a parcel's weight to print on its label. People type it in kilograms or in grams.

## What to build

- [ ] `parseWeight(text)` in `src/parcel.mjs` returns the grams of a weight typed as a number and `kg` or `g`, such as `2.5kg` or `800g`, and refuses any other text
- [ ] a test of `parseWeight`
