Give each post an address made from its title

Each post gets an address made from its title, so a reader can guess it and share it. Writers type their titles as they like, with commas, question marks and spaces.

## What to build

- [ ] `slugOf(title)` in `src/posts.mjs` returns the title's words in lower case, joined by hyphens, a word being a run of letters and digits: `my-first-post` for "My first post"
- [ ] a test of `slugOf`
