Rank the teams at the end of a quiz night

At the end of the night the quiz master reads out the teams from the most points to the fewest.

## What to build

- [ ] `ranking(scores)` in `src/quiz.mjs` returns each team with its points over every round, as `{ team, points }`, the most points first
- [ ] `node src/cli.mjs rank <file>` prints that ranking, one team a line
- [ ] a test of `ranking`
