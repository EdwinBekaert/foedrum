# 0371. A sub-order the approved plan marks test-first off brews without its recipe's test-first steps

Date: 2026-11-10 · Status: Accepted · Amends [0028](0028-brewer-runner-outcomes-environment-and-test-first.md) (a batch whose approved plan turned test-first off reads `testFirst` as false, whatever its resolved recipe says), [0233](0233-a-plan-is-an-out-doc-whose-paths-the-engine-checks-shown-as-one-comment-per-order.md) (a sub-order's `testFirst: false` acts on its batch), [0290](0290-a-sub-order-s-brewers-read-its-plan-entry-from-the-parent-s-recorded-plan.md) (the batch reads its entry's `testFirst` from the parent's recorded plan, as its brewers read the entry), [0064](0064-test-first-recipes-when-onblocked-and-a-red-proof-required.md) (a `when` reads `recipe.testFirst` as the batch has it), [0322](0322-a-sub-order-s-security-mark-is-the-brewmaster-s-and-reaches-its-plan-entry.md) (its refusal of `testFirst: false` on a marked sub-order now keeps a batch test-first), [0285](0285-an-approved-plan-becomes-brewery-sub-issues-through-the-ledger.md) (the sub-issue's body says it), [0225](0225-a-ready-pull-request-gets-a-merge-brief.md) (the merge brief gives the plan's reason for a step not run) and [0167](0167-a-brewed-pull-request-follows-one-layout-and-opens-as-a-draft-unless-every-taster-approves.md) ("How this was brewed" says it)

## Context
A plan's sub-order may say `"testFirst": false` with a reason in `testFirstOff` (ADR 0233), and the plan comment showed it as `test-first off: <reason>`.
No batch read it: a sub-order's batch ran its recipe's own `testFirst` (`resolveRecipe`, ADR 0028), fixed when the recipe loads, so a person who signed that line off got a batch that still ran the Gauger and the red proof.
Its brewers read the entry too (ADR 0290), so a Gauger could be asked for failing tests by a batch whose entry said test-first off.
#848 asked which is true: the field turns test-first off for its sub-order's batch, or it leaves the plan and the Brewmaster names a recipe that is not test-first.

## Decision
- **The approved plan's `testFirst: false` holds** (decided with the maintainer, 2026-11-10). A sub-order a person approved in the plan with `"testFirst": false` and its reason brews with test-first off: every step of its recipe whose `when` reads `recipe.testFirst` is skipped, in `nip` the four steps `survey`, `survey-check`, `gauge` and `red`. The Brewmaster only proposes it: the person's approval of the plan is what turns it off (ADR 0281).
- **One choice per batch.** A batch builds one sub-order, so test-first is on or off for the whole batch, and a plan may mix sub-orders with and without it. A sub-order without the field runs its recipe's own `testFirst`, as before, and so does an order that is no sub-order.
- **The fold reads it from the recorded plan, with no event of its own.** When it folds a sub-order's `batch.ordered`, whose `plan` names the parent's plan attempt and entry (ADR 0285), it reads that attempt's out doc, the one the approval signed, and keeps the entry's reason on the batch (`Batch.testFirstOff`). An entry it cannot find, or a plan it cannot read, leaves the batch as its recipe says.
- **A `when` reads the batch's value.** A recipe's `skip(batch, stepId)` reads `recipe.testFirst` as false for a batch with `testFirstOff`, and as the recipe resolved it otherwise. Every reader of a skip already asks it with the batch: the engine when it enters a step (ADR 0064), the merge brief, and the step runner's refusal of a test-first batch without a test map (ADR 0297), which a skipped red proof leaves alone. A recipe whose own `testFirst` is false skips the same steps, so the plan's field changes nothing there.
- **A person sees it where the steps are listed.** The status comment says each skipped step was `skipped: test-first is off in the approved plan: <reason>`; the merge brief says `not run: test-first is off in the approved plan: <reason>` for those steps and the red proof (ADR 0225); the pull request's "How this was brewed" gets `Test-first was off, as the approved plan says: <reason>. Not run: <steps>.` when the batch skipped a step. A skip of such a batch is put down to the plan also where the recipe is itself not test-first: the plan's reason is then shown though the recipe alone would have skipped the step, and both are true. The reason is the Brewmaster's text, made safe as each surface makes model text safe.
- **The plan says what the batch will do.** A sub-order's section in the plan comment, and so its plan entry (ADR 0290), says `test-first off, so its batch skips its recipe's test-first steps: <reason>`; its sub-issue's body gains `- Test-first off, so its batch skips its recipe's test-first steps: <reason>`.
- **ADR 0322's refusal stays.** A sub-order with a `securityMark` refuses `testFirst: false`, so a marked sub-order's batch stays test-first.

## Consequences
- **No migration, no driver lockstep.** No event gains a field, and a Brewery's files do not change. An older recorded plan with the field still reads.
- **A sub-order batch already ordered when this lands** from a plan that said `testFirst: false` reads the reason from its first fold after the upgrade: steps it already ran keep their attempts, and a test-first step it has not reached yet is skipped. A batch in the middle of its `gauge` attempt keeps the failing tests that attempt commits, then skips `red` and goes on to `implement`. A pull request it opened after running every test-first step, whose body a later round writes again (ADR 0227), says nothing of test-first, since the batch skipped none.
- **The step that merges the base stays the recipe's.** `mergesBase`, the last `passes: true` check the recipe runs (ADR 0164), is decided when the recipe loads from its own `testFirst`. In `nip` that is `green`, which has no `when`, so nothing changes; a recipe whose last such check reads `recipe.testFirst` would name a step a test-first-off batch skips.
- A test-first-off batch runs no Gauger, so Cellarman's `onBlocked` route back to `gauge` makes the batch Stuck as a divergence, as it does in a recipe that is not test-first (ADR 0064).
- The plan comment's line for such a sub-order is longer by the words that say what its batch does.

## Alternatives considered
- **The field leaves the plan, and the Brewmaster names a recipe that is not test-first.** A Brewery may have no such `brew` recipe, and the plan comment would no longer say why a sub-order is built without failing tests first.
- **A field on `batch.ordered` written by the intake.** Batches ordered before the change would stay as their recipe says, but every driver of a Brewery would have to be updated together, since an older one refuses a field it does not know, and the plan would be read in two places.
- **The recipe resolved per batch** (`recipes.find(name, settings)`). Every caller of `find` would change, for the one setting a `when` reads.
