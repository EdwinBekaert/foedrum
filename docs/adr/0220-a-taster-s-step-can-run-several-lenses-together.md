# 0220. A Taster's step can run several lenses together

Date: 2026-10-26 · Status: Accepted · Amends [0017](0017-test-first-recipes-and-tasting-lenses.md) (one broad Taster is now a recipe's choice), [0161](0161-lens-steps-run-a-taster-whose-findings-are-advisory-unless-the-step-blocks.md) (a lens step names one lens or several, and a finding may name its lens), [0168](0168-a-parallel-group-runs-its-lens-steps-side-by-side.md) (a group's child may be such a step), [0214](0214-a-taster-s-out-doc-says-what-risk-is-left.md) (a risk-left item of such a step starts with its lens), [0169](0169-house-yeast-rules-reach-a-batch-s-brewers-by-scope-and-lens.md) (the rules of each of a step's lenses), [0143](0143-lenses-in-two-folders-diff-and-plan.md) (each lens of a step checked against what it reviews), and [0191](0191-the-step-a-findings-route-goes-back-to-records-a-disposition-for-each-finding.md) and [0192](0192-a-findings-route-that-runs-out-of-rounds-goes-stuck-with-the-open-findings.md) (a finding's lines show its lens)

## Context
A recipe tastes through lenses one Taster each: a lens step names one `lens` ([ADR 0161](0161-lens-steps-run-a-taster-whose-findings-are-advisory-unless-the-step-blocks.md)), and a `parallel` group runs several such steps side by side ([ADR 0168](0168-a-parallel-group-runs-its-lens-steps-side-by-side.md)).
Each Taster reads the order and the whole change, so seven lenses read them seven times, and tasting is about a third of a batch's tokens (#260).
[ADR 0017](0017-test-first-recipes-and-tasting-lenses.md) rejected one broad Taster by reasoning alone ("less depth per concern"); no run compared the two.
The maintainer settled (2026-10-26, #560) that a recipe keeps many lenses, names the ones it uses, and may run them together in one Taster; #567 measures apart against together on a seeded change and sets the default recipes' choice, and #391's plan recipes `mash` and `decoction` follow it.
Every reader after a Taster's step (attempts, leases, deadline, status comment, route, rounds, retry, skip, rewind) works per step and reads its out doc's findings.

## Decision
- **How a recipe says together.** A Taster's step names `lenses`, a list of at least two lenses, each once, instead of `lens`: `{ "id": "taste", "brewer": "taster", "lenses": ["diff/correctness", "diff/acceptance"] }`. The schema refuses `lenses` beside `lens`, with one lens, and with a lens named twice (`Each lens may be named once`), each at its path; the load refuses `lenses` on a brewer that is no Taster as it refuses `lens` there, and a Taster's step with neither, names each lens neither the Brewery nor the defaults have, and checks each against what the step reviews (ADR 0143), so a `diff/` and a `plan/` lens never share a step. It is one step: one id, one attempt at a time, one lease, one deadline, one out doc, one status comment line. It stands in a line, or as a child of a parallel group beside lens steps apart, so a recipe can run security apart on another Taster and the rest together.
- **Each finding names its lens.** A finding gains `lens`, optional in the schema, so a one-lens out doc reads as before; a finding without one is its step's lens's, which `lensOfFinding` gives a reader that counts by lens (#561). The attempt's out doc schema is built for its step (`attemptLimits`, `tastingOutDocOf`): on a `lenses` step each finding must name one of the step's lenses, and on a one-lens step a finding that names one must name the step's, so no Taster labels its findings with lenses it was not given. A finding that breaks the rule makes the out doc invalid, and the attempt fails and is tried again, as any schema error is (ADR 0161). An older Foedrum cannot read an out doc, or a Stuck event's open findings, that holds a finding's `lens`, as [ADR 0191](0191-the-step-a-findings-route-goes-back-to-records-a-disposition-for-each-finding.md) records for `dispositions`.
- **One verdict.** The out doc keeps one `verdict`, from all its findings: `changes` when a finding of any lens that the change introduced is a `blocker` or `major`. `"blocking": true` on a `lenses` step blocks on any lens. A recipe that wants one lens to block and the others to advise runs that lens apart, beside the together step in a group; a `blocking` per lens is not built.
- **Model and effort** are the step's brewer's, as for every step (`modelOf`, the alias in `models.jsonc`), and so are its `maxTurns` and stall limit; the step's `deadline` can raise the brewer's. When lenses want different models, the recipe puts them in steps whose Tasters name different aliases, side by side in a group.
- **What the Taster reads.** The step's lenses reach it as its one input `lens`, ADR 0098's lens input as on a one-lens step: its text is every lens file in the step's order, and its tag's source a `lenses` source that names each lens and its file. On a `lenses` step only, the engine adds a fixed text after the instructions: review through each lens in turn, give each finding the `lens` it was found through, read a lens file's "stay inside this one" as "report under this lens only what it asks", and start each `riskLeft` item with its lens. The lens files and `defaults/brewers/taster.md` stay as they are, so a one-lens prompt is unchanged byte for byte. A step that reviews a diff is given the change once. Its house yeast is the rules each of its lenses would get apart, each rule once, in the order of its lenses, and `step.finished` lists them once (ADR 0169).
- **What reads the findings after the step.** Dispositions stay matched by place ([ADR 0191](0191-the-step-a-findings-route-goes-back-to-records-a-disposition-for-each-finding.md)): a later round's input takes the first disposition at a place not taken, so two lenses' findings at one line get one each, in order. The route input is the step's whole out doc (ADR 0128), so the step a findings route goes back to reads every lens's findings at once, where a group of lenses apart carries the first blocking child's only (ADR 0168). A later round's earlier findings, the status comment's Findings, the pull request body's `finding:` lines and the Stuck reason's open findings show a finding's lens before its severity when it names one (`diff/security, major in src/a.ts:3`), so a one-lens step's lines stay as they are. The status comment shows ten findings per lens when every finding of the step names one, as on a `lenses` step, with a count of the rest per lens, so seven lenses together show what seven apart would; otherwise ten per step, as before. The schema bounds the lenses to the recipe's, so a Taster cannot pass the cap by labelling its findings.
- **Risk left** keeps its shape: at most 10 lines per out doc ([ADR 0214](0214-a-taster-s-out-doc-says-what-risk-is-left.md)). On a `lenses` step the fixed text asks each item to start with its lens (`diff/security: …`); the engine reads nothing per lens from it, and the status comment still shows five per step.
- **The comparison is #567.** The seeded comparison (one planted defect per lens, 3 runs each of seven lenses apart, seven together, and Nip's two together) needs real `claude` runs with a login, which no test layer reaches. It is a hand-run script with a unit-tested core, as `/brew-review` on the planted samples is, and its numbers go in an ADR that amends this one and sets Nip's choice and #391's. Until then the default recipes keep their steps.

## Consequences
- A recipe can taste through several lenses for the tokens of one read of the order and the change, and choose per lens: together, apart, or apart on another model.
- A `lenses` step is one attempt: one stall or kill runs every lens again, and a retry, skip or rewind takes them all; that is the recipe's trade for the tokens.
- A step a findings route sends back to a `lenses` step reads every lens's findings in one route input, which a group of lenses apart does not give it.
- #561 counts a `lenses` step's tokens once, under its lenses together, since one attempt cannot be split by lens; its findings each name their lens.

## Alternatives considered
- **`together: true` on a parallel group.** Its children would name no attempt, against ADR 0168's "each child is a step of its own", and each child's `blocking`, `onBlocked`, status comment line, retry, skip and rewind would mean nothing.
- **A new step kind.** Another kind for the step union, `stepKind`, `walk` and the load, with the same fields as a lens step again.
- **The lens as a prefix of each finding's `why`.** Nothing could check it.
- **Findings under per-lens keys.** Every reader of a Taster's out doc would change shape.
- **On a one-lens step, `TastingOutDoc` as it is, a `lens` shown as written.** Its Taster could label its findings with lenses it was not given, and the status comment, which caps them per lens, would show every one of them (review of #571).
- **A verdict per lens in the out doc.** The engine can derive one from the findings, and a second field would have to agree with them.
- **`blocking` as a list of lenses.** One step that blocks on some lenses and advises on others; running the blocking lens apart says the same with what exists.
- **The strongest model of the step's lenses.** A lens has no model, and it would be a cost the recipe did not write.
- **`riskLeft` items of `{ lens, text }`, or 10 per lens.** A second shape for every reader and for an older Foedrum; the status comment shows five per step anyway.
- **Ten findings per step in the status comment.** Seven lenses together would show less than seven apart.
