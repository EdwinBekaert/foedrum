# 0319. Brett's survivors are listed where findings are, and a blocking Brett step sends them back

Date: 2026-11-03 · Status: Accepted · Amends [0017](0017-test-first-recipes-and-tasting-lenses.md) (where confirmed survivors show, and what blocking on them does), [0161](0161-lens-steps-run-a-taster-whose-findings-are-advisory-unless-the-step-blocks.md) (`blocking` is followed on a Brett step too), [0167](0167-a-brewed-pull-request-follows-one-layout-and-opens-as-a-draft-unless-every-taster-approves.md) (the pull request's reports list a Brett step's survivors, and a survivor makes no draft), [0185](0185-npm-run-demo-tastes-through-a-parallel-group-of-three-lenses.md) (the demo's recipe runs a Brett step after its group), [0225](0225-a-ready-pull-request-gets-a-merge-brief.md) (the brief's Risk left lists Brett's survivors too) and [0313](0313-the-engine-runs-brett-s-mutants-in-scratch-trees-against-a-fresh-baseline.md) (a survivor's record keeps its edit, and a blocking step's attempt ends blocked by its survivors)

## Context
Since [ADR 0313](0313-the-engine-runs-brett-s-mutants-in-scratch-trees-against-a-fresh-baseline.md) the engine runs Brett's mutants and records each verdict on the attempt's `step.finished`, and only `foedrum replay` shows them.
The status comment, the pull request and the merge brief list a Taster's findings, never a mutant.
A record keeps the mutant's file, line and description, not its edit, so nothing can show what the surviving bug was.
The recipe loader refused `blocking` on any step but a Taster's, since a blocking Brett step would have changed nothing.
[ADR 0017](0017-test-first-recipes-and-tasting-lenses.md) has confirmed survivors listed in the pull request and block only with `"blocking": true`.
No default recipe runs Brett: it was decided with the maintainer (2026-11-03) that a Brewery opts in with its own recipe, and that Nip gets a Brett step once runs on the ladder repositories have measured the time it takes.

## Decision
- **A survivor's record keeps its edit.** `MutantRecord` gains `original` and `replacement`, on a `survived` verdict only: the line the mutant replaced and the line that replaced it (empty to delete it), each put on one line, cut to 500 characters and left out when it looks like a secret, as the record's other texts are. Both are optional, so a store from before reads as today and its survivors are listed without their edit. A killed, timed-out, invalid or not-judged mutant is only counted, so its edit is not kept.
- **The survivors are listed where findings are** (`src/engine/survivors.ts`). For each step whose latest attempt succeeded with mutants, one line per survivor, `survived: <file>:<line>: - <original> / + <replacement>: <description>`, and one line that counts the mutants by verdict, `2 mutants: 1 survived, 1 killed`, with only the verdicts some mutant had:
  - in the status comment's **Findings**, after the Tasters' findings, under the step;
  - in the pull request's report of the step, which `stepReports` now gives for Brett's out doc too (the strict contract schema refused its `mutants` key);
  - in the merge brief's **Risk left**, each survivor, without the count.
  Every text is model or repository text and goes through the same filters as a Taster's findings: one line, no secret, no Markdown, no mention, inside the reports' fence in the pull request.
- **`blocking` on a Brett step is followed.** The loader accepts `blocking` on a step whose brewer writes the `mutants` out doc, and still refuses it on any other brewer that is no Taster. Its attempt's limits carry it to `mutated`, which, once the verdicts are in, ends an attempt with at least one survivor `blocked` by findings instead of succeeded. Each survivor becomes a finding: its file and line, `major`, introduced (a mutant lands only on a line the change added, ADR 0311), its `why` the edit and the description. The blocked summary names each survivor too, so the route input the fix step reads holds them ([ADR 0128](0128-an-attempt-a-route-schedules-gets-what-sent-it-back.md)). From there the batch goes as a blocking Taster's does: the step's `onBlocked` findings route sends it back ([ADR 0161](0161-lens-steps-run-a-taster-whose-findings-are-advisory-unless-the-step-blocks.md), [ADR 0264](0264-nip-s-correctness-taster-blocks-and-sends-its-findings-back-to-cellarman.md)), the findings are recorded on `step.finished` ([ADR 0221](0221-foedrum-lenses-reports-each-lens-s-findings-their-ends-and-its-tokens.md)), a route out of rounds leaves them open on the Stuck reason ([ADR 0192](0192-a-findings-route-that-runs-out-of-rounds-goes-stuck-with-the-open-findings.md)), and the merge brief lists a skipped one's as left open. A later round whose mutants all die, or that has none, succeeds; so does an attempt whose mutants were all invalid or not judged, since no run showed a gap, and its count line (`3 mutants: 3 not judged`) says what was left unjudged, as on an advisory step ([ADR 0313](0313-the-engine-runs-brett-s-mutants-in-scratch-trees-against-a-fresh-baseline.md): a step that ran out of time to judge did its own work).
- **Advisory by default.** Without `blocking`, the attempt succeeds whatever the verdicts, and a survivor is listed, never a finding. The draft rule ([ADR 0167](0167-a-brewed-pull-request-follows-one-layout-and-opens-as-a-draft-unless-every-taster-approves.md)) reads the Tasters' verdicts only, so a survivor never makes the pull request a draft: a blocking step's survivors hold the batch up before the pull request instead. Whether a step a person skipped while it was blocked by findings should make a draft is a question for Tasters as much as for Brett, and is an issue of its own.
- **No default recipe runs Brett.** A Brewery adds `{ "id": "mutants", "brewer": "brett" }` to its own recipe after the change's checks, with `"blocking": true` and an `onBlocked` findings route to have the survivors fixed. `npm run demo`'s recipe runs one after its taste group, advisory: a parallel group runs only Tasters ([ADR 0168](0168-a-parallel-group-runs-its-lens-steps-side-by-side.md)). Its scripted Brett proposes two mutants of the line the fix of round 1 leaves, and the engine's real runs kill one and confirm the other, which no test reaches (a name of only spaces).

## Consequences
- A Brewery that adds a Brett step sees each line its change added that its tests do not pin as a surviving mutant in the status comment, the pull request and the merge brief; with `blocking`, the fix step gets them as findings.
- A blocking Brett step costs a fix round per survivor found, and its next round runs every mutant again, within Brett's 20-minute deadline.
- The demo runs three more test runs per batch, in scratch trees.
- An event with a survivor is up to 1,000 characters longer per survivor, at most five.

## Alternatives considered
- **Pair each record with the proposed mutant in the attempt's out doc to show the edit.** The out doc is the model's, kept only on a succeeded outcome, and a record and a proposal are paired only by their order; the record is the engine's account of what it judged.
- **Keep every mutant's edit.** Only a survivor is shown; a killed mutant's edit would only grow the event.
- **Run Brett inside the taste group.** A parallel group runs Tasters' lens steps, which end at once; Brett's runs take minutes and a scratch tree each, and lifting that rule is a change of ADR 0168 of its own.
- **Make a blocking step's survivors draft the pull request.** They never reach it unskipped: a blocked step holds the batch, as a Taster's does, and a skip is a person's choice, handled as a skipped Taster's is.
