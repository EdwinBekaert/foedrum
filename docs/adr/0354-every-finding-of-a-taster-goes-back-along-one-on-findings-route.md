# 0354. Every finding of a Taster goes back along one onFindings route

Date: 2026-11-08 · Status: Accepted · Amends [0161](0161-lens-steps-run-a-taster-whose-findings-are-advisory-unless-the-step-blocks.md) (a Taster's findings are no longer advisory: every one goes back), [0168](0168-a-parallel-group-runs-its-lens-steps-side-by-side.md) (the route sits on the group, and a child blocked by findings waits for its group), [0192](0192-a-findings-route-that-runs-out-of-rounds-goes-stuck-with-the-open-findings.md) (the open findings of a group are every child's of the last round), [0264](0264-nip-s-correctness-taster-blocks-and-sends-its-findings-back-to-cellarman.md), [0306](0306-nip-tastes-through-the-security-lens-beside-correctness-and-acceptance.md), [0321](0321-mash-and-decoction-review-a-plan-through-the-plan-lenses-apart.md) and [0343](0343-nip-tastes-through-design-conformance-as-a-fourth-advisory-lens.md) (every default recipe's Tasters send their findings back), [0345](0345-a-pull-request-past-a-skip-says-so-and-an-ended-batch-s-status-comment-is-left-alone.md) (a skip names an approving Taster's open findings as it names a blocking one's) [0349](0349-a-group-s-round-back-carries-every-finding-of-the-round.md) (the route input heads each child by its verdict), and [0224](0224-the-default-recipes-taste-apart-or-together-as-a-seeded-comparison-measured.md), [0323](0323-a-seeded-plan-measures-the-plan-lenses-apart-and-together-after-the-merge.md) and [0332](0332-tasting-compare-real-re-tastes-brewed-heads-and-writes-blinded-lists.md) (a measured run ends after its first tasting round)

## Context
Only a step marked `blocking` sent its findings back ([ADR 0161](0161-lens-steps-run-a-taster-whose-findings-are-advisory-unless-the-step-blocks.md)).
In `nip` that was correctness; acceptance, security and design conformance advised, and an advisory finding waited for a person to read it in the status comment or the pull request.
The maintainer's rule is that a finding never waits for a person.
On #948 every Taster approved with two minor findings, a real gap and a stale design page, and nothing acted on them; a session fixed both by hand.
On #924 design conformance found a design page left stale, and it became a follow-up issue (#925).
A round back already carries every finding of the group's round to the Cellarman, which fixes or dismisses each ([ADR 0349](0349-a-group-s-round-back-carries-every-finding-of-the-round.md)); what was missing is a round when nothing blocked.
Each blocking child also had a route and rounds of its own, so `mash`'s coverage and sizing could send a plan back four times.

Decided with the maintainer on #926 (2026-11-08); which Tasters run again after a round (#950) and pre-existing findings as Brewery issues (#951) are decided there.

## Decision
- **`onFindings` on the group.** A parallel group of Tasters names where their findings go: `"onFindings": { "goto": "implement", "maxRounds": 2 }`, a route like `onFail`, to a step before the group. A Taster's step on its own names the same field on the step. A group has no `onFail` or `onBlocked`: no use case.
- **A Taster's findings always have a route.** The loader refuses, each with a line that names the fix: a group of Tasters without `onFindings`; a Taster's step on its own without one; `blocking` or `onBlocked` on a Taster's step; `onFindings` on a child of a group, which is the group's; and `onFindings` on a step whose brewer is no Taster. Brett keeps `blocking` and `onBlocked` ([ADR 0319](0319-brett-s-survivors-are-listed-where-findings-are-and-a-blocking-brett-step-sends-them-back.md)), and the Cellarman keeps its `onBlocked` route to the Gauger for a wrong test.
- **Every finding goes back.** The runner settles a routed Taster's `done` with any finding, whatever its verdict, as blocked by findings: `the Taster asks for changes: …` on a `changes` verdict, `the Taster approves with findings: …` on an approval. A finding with `introduced: false` goes back too: the Cellarman marks it `pre-existing` ([ADR 0191](0191-the-step-a-findings-route-goes-back-to-records-a-disposition-for-each-finding.md)), and the next round does not raise it again. A done Taster with no finding succeeds.
- **The group decides once every child ended.** A child blocked by findings waits for its siblings, whatever rounds are left. Then, when any child was blocked, the batch goes back once along the group's route, recorded as from the first such child in the recipe's order, since the route input reads that child's out doc and the round's other findings from it ([ADR 0349](0349-a-group-s-round-back-carries-every-finding-of-the-round.md)). The whole group still runs again after the round (#950 decides otherwise).
- **Rounds are shared, two in all in every default recipe.** A route counts the attempts of its `goto` step that any child of the group sent back. After them, findings still open make the batch Stuck, as correctness's did ([ADR 0192](0192-a-findings-route-that-runs-out-of-rounds-goes-stuck-with-the-open-findings.md)): the Stuck reason names the first blocked child and its summary, and its open findings are every blocked child's of the last round, ten at most with a count of the rest. Open is any finding of that round: a contested dismissal, a fix that did not hold, or a new finding, since a Taster never raises a disposed finding again as it was (ADR 0191). Fixed and uncontested dismissed findings are listed in the merge brief, as they were.
- **The default recipes.** `nip`'s group goes back to `implement`, `mash`'s and `decoction`'s to `plan`, each with `maxRounds` 2; every plan lens's findings go back, the three that advised before too.
- **What a person decides.** No separate step: the Cellarman's prompt says that a finding it cannot settle, because it needs a decision it cannot make from the order, the code or a decision record, ends its attempt with its questions (`needs_input`). Its `version` goes to 14. The Brewmaster's prompt already ends with questions on an open decision, and is unchanged.
- **The route input** heads each other child of the round by its verdict, `asks for changes`, `approves with findings` or `approves` with no findings, where it said `blocking` or `advisory`, those that asked for changes first. An older Foedrum's advisory child that succeeded with a `changes` verdict reads as `asks for changes`.
- **`skip`.** A skip goes past every step whose latest attempt did not succeed ([ADR 0345](0345-a-pull-request-past-a-skip-says-so-and-an-ended-batch-s-status-comment-is-left-alone.md)); an approving Taster with findings now ends blocked by them, so its line says it is left open by a skip, as a blocking one's did.
- **`node scripts/tasting-compare.ts`.** Its recipes route their Tasters back to the scripted step, `maxRounds` 1, and that step's next turn ends blocked with the summary `tasting-compare ends the run after its first tasting round`; the record reads that Stuck as `after round 1`, which is no failure. A Taster blocked by its findings counts as a review: its findings are the ones its `step.finished` recorded, the first 100, each cut to 1,000 characters, and a record that dropped some fails the run; its `riskLeft` is not on the event, so a measured Taster with findings shows none. A run measures round 1 alone, at the Taster sessions it had before.

## Consequences
- Every finding is acted on before a person sees the batch: fixed, dismissed with evidence, marked pre-existing, or open on a Stuck batch after two rounds.
- A batch with a minor finding costs one more Cellarman attempt, the Finer and green again, and a second tasting round of every Taster. On #924 that round would have been about 1.6M tokens on top of 2.86M, added up from its receipts; #950 cuts it to correctness and the Tasters that had findings.
- Risk, not measured: a Taster that raises a new minor finding in every round makes the batch Stuck after two rounds. The next brews measure how often.
- A batch in flight whose log has rounds from more than one child counts all of them as the group's: a `mash` batch that went back once for coverage and once for sizing has used its two rounds. No Brewery has such a batch open that the maintainer knows of.
- A child blocked by findings no longer makes the batch Stuck while its siblings still run, so a skip after the rounds stops none of them.
- A recipe can no longer start with a Taster's step or a group of them: there is no step before it to go back to.
- The pull request and the status comment list a Taster's findings only for an attempt that succeeded with some, which only an older Foedrum's advisory step did; the draft line for a Taster that asked for changes stays for those.
- **Migration: none.** The only Brewery has no recipes of its own, so it brews with the defaults, which this change moves. A Brewery recipe with a Taster's step now fails to load until it has `onFindings`, and `foedrum doctor` shows the loader's line naming the fix.

## Alternatives considered
- **`onBlocked` on a Taster's step on its own:** rejected by the maintainer, one word wherever a Taster stands.
- **Keep the route on each child, rounds of their own:** rejected; the batch went back once per blocking child, four times for `mash`'s two, and an advisory child's findings never went back.
- **The route recorded from the group:** rejected; the route input reads the routing attempt's out doc and the round's other findings from a child, so the round is recorded from the first blocked child, and the rounds are counted over every child.
- **A step of its own for a person's decision:** rejected by the maintainer; the Cellarman asks when it cannot settle a finding.
- **A check that a fix round changed nothing else:** rejected until a brew shows the need; the Tasters read the whole change again.
- **The harness reading round 1 of a run that goes on:** rejected; a second round would add a Cellarman pass and a Taster session per lens to every measured run with findings, for nothing the comparison reads.
