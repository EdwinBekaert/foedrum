# 0337. A Taster's first call on a diff reads the instruction file, and a long risk line is cut

Date: 2026-11-06 · Status: Accepted · Amends [0331](0331-a-taster-on-a-diff-reads-the-repository-s-rules-as-its-standard.md) (how the default Taster is told to read the rules, and what they cost), [0214](0214-a-taster-s-out-doc-says-what-risk-is-left.md) (a `riskLeft` line past its limit no longer fails the attempt), [0088](0088-out-doc-text-that-looks-like-a-secret-is-left-out-of-the-pull-request-and-the-status-comment.md) (such a line is filtered when the out doc is read, before its event is recorded) and [0332](0332-tasting-compare-real-re-tastes-brewed-heads-and-writes-blinded-lists.md) (a third setup, with a recipe of its own) · Amended by [0343](0343-nip-tastes-through-design-conformance-as-a-fourth-advisory-lens.md), [0344](0344-tasting-compare-real-measures-a-stronger-model-and-more-effort-apart.md), [0347](0347-tasting-compare-real-measures-tasters-without-the-rules-read-and-three-lenses-together.md) and [0352](0352-nip-s-correctness-acceptance-and-security-tasters-run-at-high-effort-without-the-rules-read.md)

## Context
ADR 0331 gave the default Taster a section on reading the repository's rules.
#879's first round measured it: 0 of 9 Tasters opened `CLAUDE.md`, a rule file, a decision record or a design page, the same as the Taster before it.
The section was in their prompts, and its tests only pin its words.

A second try, by hand and on #864 and #867 only, made the instruction the first bullet and asked the out doc's body to name what was read.
13 of 13 attempts opened `CLAUDE.md`, and none opened a decision record or design page.
The bug at `driver.ts:1230` on #867 was named as a finding in both goes.

One attempt in that try failed with `Too big: expected string to have <=300 characters → at riskLeft[0]`, with its review finished, and the prompt already stated the limit.
An invalid out doc costs the attempt its work, and longer reviews will meet this more often.

## Decision
- **On a diff, the first tool call reads the instruction file.** It is the first bullet of the default Taster's rules, before "Read the change first", in the words that were tried, whatever the lens and however small the change. [`taster.md`](../../defaults/brewers/taster.md) holds them. "Read the change first" and the section's "Once {change} shows what the change touches" stay as they are, since the bullet was tried beside them: {change} is in the prompt, so reading it takes no tool call unless it was cut.
- **On a diff, the body starts with a `Rules read:` line**, naming each rule file, decision record and design page read. It replaces "In the body, say what you read…", and what follows it still says what was read and checked through the lens. The engine reads no out doc's body for this and checks nothing: it is the Taster's own account, kept for whoever reads the attempt. `tasting-compare --real` writes it under `bodies/` (ADR 0332).
- **A repository with no instruction file.** "Your first tool call reads" now produces a failed read there. The prompt says that is no finding and no failure, and the line then says `Rules read: none in this repository`. A failed read adds one to the receipt's `toolErrors`, a count that nothing reads to decide an outcome, and it is no denial, so `maxDenials` does not see it.
- **A plan Taster's rules stay as they are.** Its prompt carries both lines, since the file's body is one text (ADR 0331), and each opens with "On a diff". It still reads the instructions when its lens asks.
- **A `riskLeft` line past 300 characters is cut when the out doc is read, and the attempt goes on.** `TastingOutDoc` keeps the line's start and ends it with ` [cut: the rest is past the limit of 300 characters]`, 300 characters in all, so what is stored and shown stays within the limit. The cut stands in the stored output, so everything that reads the output later, and an earlier round's out doc read again, sees the same line. The out doc file itself is left as written. It holds for a plan Taster and for a Brewery's own brewer of the `tasting` schema too, since they share the schema. The prompt adds: "A longer line is cut, so put what matters first."
- **A line is read for a secret before it is cut**, since a cut can break a secret in two and hide what is left of it from `withoutSecrets`, the reason #883 gives for its own cut. It reads the line's first 1,000 characters, what the cut keeps and room for a match that runs past it, the bound the status comment has for the same reason (ADR 0088): an out doc may hold 1 MiB, and a line of it read whole could hold the event loop. A line that reads like one becomes `[left out: a riskLeft line past its limit that looks like a secret]`, which names no step, since the schema knows none. A secret past those 1,000 characters is cut away with the rest and never stored, as ADR 0088 has it for what is never shown. One within them but past the cut costs the line too, the simpler rule, as it does on the status comment. No commit is known where the schema reads, so a full commit hash in that part of the line gets the fixed line too, and the risk that line named is lost. A line within the limit is read as before, by the renderers, which know the commits.
- **This is a filter at the out doc's read, for these lines only.** ADR 0088 left that to later, since the event log is what a person repairs a batch from. A line past its limit could not reach the log at all before this, since it failed the attempt, so the log loses nothing it held. The full line stays in the out doc file.
- **The other bounds stay.** A list of more than 10 items, an item of two lines or an empty one still makes the out doc invalid, and so does every other out doc's limit: the issue's failure was the length of a line, and no other out doc is cut when it is read. What reads out doc text elsewhere cuts it on the way out (the status comment, a pull request's cells) and reads for a secret first, within the same kind of bound.
- The Taster's `version` goes to 9. Nothing compares a brewer's version (ADR 0331).
- **The comparison gets a third setup, `lens`, on all three orders**, named like `855-lens` and decided by the maintainer: `nip`'s three lenses and `diff/design-conformance` as a fourth Taster, with the default Taster. ADR 0332's one recipe becomes two, `real-nip` for `today`, `reads` and ADR 0333's `867-comments`, and `real-nip-lens` for `lens`, named per setup by `realRecipeOf`. A `lens` run holds no comment on its order, so it differs from `reads` by the Taster alone. One test still holds `real-nip`'s lenses to `defaults/recipes/nip.jsonc`, and one holds `real-nip-lens` to exactly those and the one. ADR 0331 and #879 point a miss on a stale page at the lens first, and this measures that.
- **`defaults/recipes/nip.jsonc` does not change here.** Whether the lens joins `nip` is decided from this comparison.

## Consequences
- **Cost, measured.** This replaces ADR 0331's estimate of roughly double. Against the Taster before #876, the second try cost about 4 times the tokens on #864 and about 2 times on #867. It multiplies by the Tasters of a step and by the rounds, and `nip` runs three.
- **Whether it finds more is not shown here.** The tests pin the prompt's words, as #876's did, and those passed while 0 of 9 Tasters followed them. The second try is two orders, by hand, and reads a first bullet's effect, not a finding rate. #879's `tasting-compare --real`, with its key and bar unchanged, is the measure, and the maintainer runs it after the merge.
- **The `lens` setup is not blind, and costs more.** A run under it lists a design conformance Taster, so its code shows its setup to whoever scores the list; ADR 0332 already says a list cannot be wholly blind. It adds 36 Taster sessions at `--runs 3`: 3 orders, 3 runs, 4 Tasters. No number exists when this record is accepted: the maintainer runs it after the merge.
- **The second try opened no decision record or design page**, and this does not change that: the first bullet names only the instruction file. A miss in #879 on a stale page or a missing record stays where ADR 0331 points, at the lens first.
- **A cut line can lose the part that matters.** ADR 0214 rejected a cut for that reason, when the second attempt looked cheap. The measured reviews make the attempt the dearer loss, and the mark says a line was cut. The status comment shows at most 200 characters of an item, so the mark is out of sight there, and the pull request's report and the merge brief show it.
- **Migration: no file in a Brewery has to change.** A Brewery that replaced `brewers/taster.md` keeps its prompt and gets none of the prompt's lines until it adds them. `foedrum doctor`'s notice on what a Brewery file drops lists a default's headings (ADR 0141), and none is new, so it does not name these. The cut needs nothing: it is in the schema. The lines to add:
  - as the first bullet: ``- **On a diff, your first tool call reads the repository's instruction file**: `CLAUDE.md` at the root of your working directory, or `AGENTS.md` where that is what it has. Do this before anything else, whatever your lens and however small the change; the section below says what else to read and what these files are.`` (the section is `## The repository's rules`, ADR 0331);
  - in place of the sentence on the body: ``On a diff, your out doc's body starts with a line `Rules read:` that names each rule file, decision record and design page you read, or says `Rules read: none in this repository`. After it, and on a plan from its start, say what you read and what you checked through the lens.``
  - in the section, in place of its last sentence on a repository with none: ``A repository with none of these goes on without them: the failed read of its instruction file is no finding and no failure, and your body's line says `Rules read: none in this repository`.``

## Alternatives considered
- **Leave the first bullet out and strengthen the section.** The section was what 0 of 9 followed.
- **Have the engine pass the instruction file as an input.** ADR 0331 rejected it and named this as the place to go next if #879 shows the self-edit gap matters. The first bullet was enough for the read itself.
- **Make the engine check the `Rules read:` line, or the read.** It would fail a finished review over a line, the failure this record removes, and a line says nothing of how a file was used.
- **Cut when a renderer shows the line, where the commits are known.** A full commit hash would survive, but three renderers would each hold the cut, the stored list would pass its limit, and a reader of the output would have to know that.
- **Read the whole line for a secret.** A line of one MiB with many matches could hold the event loop, and what lies past the first 1,000 characters is neither stored nor shown.
- **Raise the limit.** It moves the failure, and the merge brief and the pull request show every line.
- **Cut a list past 10 items too.** No attempt has failed for it, and which items to keep is a judgement a cut cannot make.
