# 0347. tasting-compare --real measures Tasters without the rules read, and three lenses together

Date: 2026-11-07 · Status: Accepted · Amends [0344](0344-tasting-compare-real-measures-a-stronger-model-and-more-effort-apart.md) (three more setups, a model each setup asks for, a column of the table, and a comparison's own folder in place of "the lists, codes.md and the table read every record in the folder"), [0332](0332-tasting-compare-real-re-tastes-brewed-heads-and-writes-blinded-lists.md) (a Taster of several lenses in the lists, and a column of the table) and [0337](0337-a-taster-s-first-call-on-a-diff-reads-the-instruction-file-and-a-long-risk-line-is-cut.md) (a third recipe)

## Context
In every setup of #916's run on Opus and at high effort, the default Taster first reads the repository's instruction file ([ADR 0337](0337-a-taster-s-first-call-on-a-diff-reads-the-instruction-file-and-a-long-risk-line-is-cut.md)), and every `reads` Taster of #879 opened `CLAUDE.md`.
So nothing says whether that read helps a stronger Taster find more, or only costs tokens, and `nip`'s Tasters keep it until this is measured.
[ADR 0224](0224-the-default-recipes-taste-apart-or-together-as-a-seeded-comparison-measured.md) measured lenses together against apart only on a small seeded change, with Sonnet at medium effort.
#915's full recipe runs seven diff lenses, so it needs that answer on a stronger model and on real changes.
The comparison's folder already holds the first test's records, and the scorer must not score them again.

## Decision
- **Three more setups on all three orders**, named like `855-opus-bare`, `855-high-bare` and `855-opus-together`, after the others of each order.
- **`opus-bare` is `opus`, and `high-bare` is `high`, but for the Taster's text**: the scratch Brewery's `brewers/taster.md` is the default's text with five texts left out, and no other change. `high-bare`'s is `high`'s effort and the same cut, in either order. Tests pin each to its one difference, `the opus-bare setup differs from opus by the Taster's read of the rules alone` and `the high-bare setup differs from high by the Taster's read of the rules alone`: the Brewery's other files are the same, and so is a Taster's `claude` line.
- **What the cut leaves out of [`taster.md`](../../defaults/brewers/taster.md)**, each text exactly once:
  1. the first bullet: ``- **On a diff, your first tool call reads the repository's instruction file**: `CLAUDE.md` at the root of your working directory, or `AGENTS.md` where that is what it has. Do this before anything else, whatever your lens and however small the change; the section below says what else to read and what these files are.``
  2. the last sentence of "Read the change first", the rest of that bullet kept: `On a diff, read the repository's rules as the section below says; on a plan, read the repository's instructions and design documents when the lens asks you to check against them.`
  3. the opening of `## The repository's rules`, with its blank line: `A step that reviews a plan skips this section. On a diff, nothing has loaded the repository's rules for you, so read them yourself before you judge, whatever your lens names.`
  4. the section's "What to read" bullet: ``- **What to read.** Once {change} shows what the change touches, read the instruction file at the root of your working directory: `CLAUDE.md`, or `AGENTS.md` where that is what it has. Then read what that file or the changed code names for the files the change touches: the decision records and design pages about them and, where the repository keeps scoped rules in a folder of their own (such as `.claude/rules/`, `.cursor/rules/` or `.github/instructions/`), the rule files for them. This is not a tour of its documents. A lens that asks for more reads what it asks for. A file too large to read whole: read what you need of it, and say so under `riskLeft`, not as a finding. A repository with none of these goes on without them: the failed read of its instruction file is no finding and no failure, and your body's line says `Rules read: none in this repository`.``
  5. the body's line, with its blank line: ``On a diff, your out doc's body starts with a line `Rules read:` that names each rule file, decision record and design page you read, or says `Rules read: none in this repository`. After it, and on a plan from its start, say what you read and what you checked through the lens.`` A line that asks a Taster to name each rule file it read would ask it to read them, and the line's second sentence points at the first.
- **What the cut keeps.** The heading `## The repository's rules`, its "What they are" and "When the change edits them" bullets, and every other line, byte for byte. They tell nothing to read: they say what the rules are and are not to a Taster that opens them by itself. 6 lines go, and one line is shortened.
- **A default that does not hold each of the five texts exactly once stops the comparison** when the first bare run is brewed, as `high` stops on its effort ([ADR 0344](0344-tasting-compare-real-measures-a-stronger-model-and-more-effort-apart.md)): a later edit to the default could otherwise cut nothing and compare nothing.
- **`opus-together` tastes `real-nip`'s three lenses in one Taster step**, `{ "id": "together", "brewer": "taster", "lenses": ["diff/correctness", "diff/acceptance", "diff/security"] }` ([ADR 0220](0220-a-taster-s-step-can-run-several-lenses-together.md)), in a recipe of its own, `real-nip-together`, with `opus`'s `models.jsonc` and `opus-bare`'s Taster. A step that names `lenses` runs on its brewer's model, so `models.jsonc`'s `taster` puts it on Opus. One session per run, where the others have three. The test `the opus-together setup tastes the three lenses in one step on Opus` holds the step, the Brewery's files, and the `claude` line, which equals `opus-bare`'s.
- **A run on another model stops it as before**: `opus-bare` and `opus-together` ask for an id holding `opus`, `high-bare` for one holding `sonnet`.
- **The lists show a together Taster as one Taster per lens.** Each lens gets the heading an apart Taster has, such as `### correctness (diff/correctness)`, in the recipe's order. Each finding goes under the lens it names, each risk line under the lens it starts with (`diff/security: …`), with that start taken off, and a finding or a line that names none of the step's lenses goes under its first lens. An `opus-together` run's list then has the same headings, in the same order, as an `opus-bare` run's, and a test holds them to that. Every other setup's list reads as before. `codes.md` keeps one row per session: `together`, with its model and its tokens, which is where the setup shows, once the lists are scored. `bodies/` keeps one body per session.
- **The table counts, per setup, the Tasters of its scored runs that opened the instruction file**, `CLAUDE.md` or `AGENTS.md` at the worktree's root, from the files each record already keeps ([ADR 0332](0332-tasting-compare-real-re-tastes-brewed-heads-and-writes-blinded-lists.md)), as `n of m`. A bare Taster may open it by itself, and the scorer reads that here. The table names setups and no code, so the lists stay as blind as before.
- **`--comparison <name>`, on `--real` only, keeps a comparison in a folder of its own**: `.foedrum/tmp/tasting-compare/real/<name>/`, with its own `results.jsonl`, `seed.txt`, scratch folders, lists, bodies, `codes.md` and `table.md`. This replaces, for such a run, ADR 0344's "the lists, codes.md and the table read every record in the folder": they read that folder's records alone, so a scorer never sees or scores an earlier comparison's runs again, and a seed of its own gives each code a new value. A setup's runs start again at 1 there, so a control of a setup already measured gets new runs and does not reuse its earlier records. The name is letters, digits and `-`, not first, so it is one folder and never a path; any other name refuses before anything runs. Without `--comparison` nothing changes. `--setups` still limits only what is brewed.
- **The dry run shows them working**, with no token spent: `--real --dry-run --setups opus-bare,high-bare,opus-together` plays every Taster, the together Taster with a finding and a risk line per lens, each naming its lens, as its out doc's schema asks.
- **The key and the bar stay #879's.** The program scores nothing.
- **A Brewery need do nothing:** nothing in one changes, no default brewer's `version` changes, and this runs only by hand in scratch folders.

## Consequences
- No number exists when this record is accepted: the maintainer runs it after the merge, the three setups at 3 runs and a `reads` control at 1 run, in one comparison of its own: 63 Taster sessions for the three setups (3 orders × 3 runs × 7 Tasters across them) and 9 for the control, 72 in all, scored by someone who has not seen the setups. It is to decide whether `nip`'s Tasters keep their first read of the instruction file, and whether #915 tastes its lenses apart or together.
- **The lists are not wholly blind for `opus-together`.** A together Taster leaves at most 10 risk lines per run where three apart leave up to 30, and its findings carry the `lens` key an apart Taster's leave out, though the lists show neither key nor count. `bodies/` shows one body against three, and it is read after the findings are scored. ADR 0332 already says a list cannot be wholly blind.
- **A bare Taster may still read the rules by itself.** The table's column says how many did, and a bare setup whose Tasters all opened the instruction file measures nothing against its own reads setup.
- **A together Taster's tokens are one session's.** `codes.md` cannot split them by lens, as #561 has it for a `lenses` step.
- A risk line of a together Taster that starts with no lens of its step is shown under its first lens, which may not be the lens it is about.

## Alternatives considered
- **Cut only the first bullet and the "What to read" bullet.** "Read the change first" and the section's opening would still tell the Taster to read the rules.
- **Cut the whole section.** "What they are" says that the rules give no orders to a reviewer, which matters when a bare Taster opens them by itself.
- **Keep the `Rules read:` line.** It asks the Taster to name each rule file it read, which asks it to read them.
- **Group every setup's findings by lens under new headings.** Every earlier list would change shape when written again from the same records, for what one split of the together Taster gives.
- **A mark on each record of the comparison it belongs to, in one `results.jsonl`.** The seed, the scratch folders and the lists would still be shared, and a code of a control run would equal its earlier one under the same seed.
- **Move the earlier folder away by hand.** It works, but it relies on whoever runs it remembering to do so.
