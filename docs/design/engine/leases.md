# Leases

Leases live in a separate mutable table (`attempt_key, owner, expires_at`; a lease covers the attempt's session as well): they are renewed every few seconds.
An attempt that ended releases its lease, also when its finish is refused because Stuck abandoned it: a step runs one attempt at a time, so a second live lease of a step is an attempt that no longer runs (I2).
A batch runs several attempts at once only in a parallel group, one per child, each a step of its own ([ADR 0168](../../adr/0168-a-parallel-group-runs-its-lens-steps-side-by-side.md)).
A driver starts no attempt of a step while another attempt of it holds a live lease, one that Stuck abandoned and whose stopped run has not ended: it starts it once that lease is released or expired, which the heartbeat no longer renews; or a finished attempt that `foedrum resume` holds and renews while its session runs (ADR 0126); nor while another driver holds a live lease of that same attempt, which a driver that lost the batch to a takeover was starting, read in the transaction that would lease it ([ADR 0124](../../adr/0124-foedrum-takeover-hands-a-batch-to-another-driver-as-a-recorded-command.md)); the attempt's deadline runs through the wait ([ADR 0055](../../adr/0055-a-step-waits-for-the-lease-of-its-abandoned-attempt.md)), and the driver says once per waiting attempt which attempt holds the lease and when it expires (`waiting_for_lease`, #155).
Once expired, a lease is released; when its attempt is still the started, running latest attempt of its step, the supervisor treats that attempt as lost, one per batch and tick, and keeps the expired leases of the batch's other lost attempts for the ticks after ([lifecycle](lifecycle.md)).
A driver that starts on a store sets the leases its own earlier run left to expire at once (`expireOwnLeases`, `src/engine/leases.ts`): their processes died with that run, so the supervisor's next ticks lose those attempts, one per batch and tick, and no new attempt of a step runs beside one of them ([ADR 0033](../../adr/0033-chaos-tests-effects-found-by-key-and-leases-at-start.md)).
It does so through `startDriver`, only under its own driver lock, whose owner it takes, so a second process with the same owner cannot expire the live leases of the first ([ADR 0037](../../adr/0037-attempt-worktrees-in-the-engine-and-driver-locks.md)).
An attempt key is the canonical JSON text `["batchId","stepId",attempt]` (`src/engine/attempt-key.ts`); the store refuses a lease under any other key (`AttemptKeyText` in `src/schemas/store.ts`), so a runner that writes a wrong one finds out at once instead of when the lease expires.
The store lists both the leases alive and those expired at a given time, so the supervisor can tell which attempt an expired lease held ([ADR 0027](../../adr/0027-supervisor-lost-and-stalled-attempts.md)).

A batch lease says which driver drives a batch until when (`batch_id, owner, expires_at`, the owner being the driver's lock owner).
`holdBatch` (`src/engine/batch-leases.ts`) takes a free or expired one, renews its owner's and refuses another driver's live one, in a transaction, so two drivers cannot both take a batch.
The driver renews it on every tick, never the brewer runner, so a driver that dies frees its batches once their leases expire, even while an orphaned brewer runs on.
With it the driver renews the lease of each attempt its runner runs, but not of one the log shows finished or abandoned, whose run goes on only until the driver stops it ([ADR 0051](../../adr/0051-chaos-runs-on-the-real-driver-with-two-drivers-and-a-terminal.md)).
Unlike an attempt lease, a batch lease is not expired at a driver's start: a driver that starts again under its owner drives on the batches it held ([ADR 0044](../../adr/0044-batch-leases-held-by-the-driver-and-one-command-log.md)).
A driver's step boundaries are pushed by its supervisor's next tick; a terminal command's events wait in the store until a driver's tick pushes them.
