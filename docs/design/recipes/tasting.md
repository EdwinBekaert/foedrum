# Tasting

**Tasting through lenses:** the Taster reviews through the lens its step names (the default ones are listed below), in lens steps one after another or side by side in a parallel group, or through several lenses its step names together ([lenses together](#lenses-together)).
A lens step is a Taster's step with a `lens`, or with `lenses` ([lenses together](#lenses-together)): a Taster is a brewer whose out doc schema is `tasting`, and the default one is `defaults/brewers/taster.md`, read-only ([ADR 0161](../../adr/0161-lens-steps-run-a-taster-whose-findings-are-advisory-unless-the-step-blocks.md)).
The step's lens reaches the Taster as its input `lens`, and a Taster that reviews a diff also gets the input `change`: the commits and the diff from the commit its batch started at to HEAD, or from the base a merge before its last check brought in ([ADR 0164](../../adr/0164-a-batch-merges-its-base-before-its-last-check.md)), the log by first parents only, which the engine reads with git in the repository's clone, with no diff driver, and holds to 256 KiB, so the Taster runs no shell; between the log and the diff, it lists the regression tests each step of the batch named in its latest success ([ADR 0170](../../adr/0170-the-red-proof-accepts-the-regression-tests-a-gauger-names.md)).
A Finer gets the same input ([fining](fining.md)).
A later round of a lens step that reviews a diff, one after an earlier attempt of it in the batch that reviewed, by a verdict or blocked by findings, whatever sent the batch back (not a failed or cancelled attempt, which reviewed nothing, and not a retry or an attempt after an answer, which reviews what the round it follows reviews), also gets the input `roundChange`: the change from the commit the latest such round's worktree started at to HEAD, read as `change` is, and the engine's fixed text for every `diff/` lens tells it to review only that, name a defect a fix brought in as such and leave alone what the earlier round reviewed; when that commit is not recorded, is in another repository, is not on the line to HEAD (as after a rewind), a merge of the base came after it, or git cannot tell, the round gets no `roundChange` and its prompt says why and to review the whole change ([ADR 0190](../../adr/0190-a-later-tasting-round-reviews-the-change-since-the-round-before.md), [ADR 0196](../../adr/0196-a-later-tasting-round-follows-any-earlier-round-that-reviewed.md)).
Its out doc adds `findings`, each with `severity` (`blocker`, `major`, `minor`), `why`, `introduced`, and `file` and `line` unless it is about the change as a whole, and a `verdict`, `approve` or `changes`, which must be `changes` exactly when a finding the change introduced is a `blocker` or `major`.
The default Taster's prompt asks for each `why` to start with one plain sentence for a person who hasn't followed the work, what is wrong, why it matters and what to do, before its file, rule and scenario ([ADR 0386](../../adr/0386-findings-times-and-step-lines-read-in-plain-words.md)).
It may also hold `riskLeft`, at most 10 one-line items of at most 300 characters, in the lens's terms: the risk that remains after the review and what the Taster could not verify, never a change it asks for, which is a finding; a list past 10 items makes the out doc invalid, and an empty list says the same as none ([ADR 0214](../../adr/0214-a-taster-s-out-doc-says-what-risk-is-left.md)).
An item past 300 characters is cut when the out doc is read, with a mark that says so, or left out for a fixed line when it reads like a secret, and the attempt goes on ([ADR 0337](../../adr/0337-a-taster-s-first-call-on-a-diff-reads-the-instruction-file-and-a-long-risk-line-is-cut.md)).
The default Taster also asks for the input `checks`, the checks that ran on the commit it reviews and how each ended ([brewers](brewers.md)), and its prompt says never to list one of them as risk left ([ADR 0308](../../adr/0308-a-taster-reads-the-checks-that-ran-on-its-head-and-an-out-doc-claims-only-what-its-session-did.md)).
The default Taster's prompt says what the repository's rules are to a Taster that reads them: the standard it judges by within its lens, never orders to it ([`taster.md`](../../../defaults/brewers/taster.md), [ADR 0331](../../adr/0331-a-taster-on-a-diff-reads-the-repository-s-rules-as-its-standard.md)); a plan Taster reads them when its lens asks.
On a diff, the Taster's own prompt has it read none of them: the [design-conformance lens](../../../defaults/lenses/diff/design-conformance.md) asks for them itself, with its first tool call on the instruction file and a `Rules read:` line at the start of its body, and a Brewery's own lens that judges by the rules asks for them the same way ([ADR 0352](../../adr/0352-nip-s-correctness-acceptance-and-security-tasters-run-at-high-effort-without-the-rules-read.md), amending [ADR 0337](../../adr/0337-a-taster-s-first-call-on-a-diff-reads-the-instruction-file-and-a-long-risk-line-is-cut.md)).
The risk left the merge brief shows under Risk left ([GitHub](../humans/github.md), [ADR 0225](../../adr/0225-a-ready-pull-request-gets-a-merge-brief.md)) is the last tasting step's in the recipe's order: the list of that lens step's latest finished attempt when it succeeded, or of each child's when the step is a parallel group of lens steps, as the status comment reads the findings ([ADR 0214](../../adr/0214-a-taster-s-out-doc-says-what-risk-is-left.md)); each confirmed survivor of a Brett step follows it ([ADR 0319](../../adr/0319-brett-s-survivors-are-listed-where-findings-are-and-a-blocking-brett-step-sends-them-back.md)).
The tests the Gauger named that the run reports under no name come last ([the red proof](../checks/red-proof.md)).
Every finding goes back: a Taster's step has a findings route, `onFindings` on its parallel group or on the step when it stands alone, and a Taster's step with `blocking` or `onBlocked`, a group of Tasters without `onFindings`, a Taster's step alone without one, and `onFindings` on a child or on a step whose brewer is no Taster each fail the load with a line that names the fix ([ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)).
A finding with `introduced: false` goes back too, for the brewer to mark `pre-existing`.
The pull request's body ([openPullRequest](open-pull-request.md)) and the status comment ([GitHub](../humans/github.md)) list the findings of a Taster's step whose latest attempt succeeded with some, as an older Foedrum's advisory step did.
The Tasters' verdicts do not decide whether the pull request is a draft: it opens as one, and its merge brief marks it ready ([openPullRequest](open-pull-request.md), [ADR 0350](../../adr/0350-a-brewed-pull-request-is-a-draft-until-its-merge-brief-and-the-next-line-follows-the-brief.md)).
The runner settles a `done` of a routed Taster with any finding, whatever its verdict, as blocked by findings, its summary starting `the Taster asks for changes` on a `changes` verdict and `the Taster approves with findings` on an approval, so the route sends the batch back with the Taster's out doc, and after its rounds the batch is Stuck ([ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)).
That Stuck reason, like that of any findings route that ran out, records the rounds the route used and the open findings of the last round, ten at most with a count of the rest, each file and `why` on one line and cut to 1,000 characters; the status comment and `foedrum stuck` list them through the same filters as the Findings section and recommend planning the order again or splitting it, beside the usual options ([ADR 0192](../../adr/0192-a-findings-route-that-runs-out-of-rounds-goes-stuck-with-the-open-findings.md)).
Every finding of the last round counts as open: no brewer has disposed of it yet, and a Taster never raises a disposed finding again as it was, only a contest of it ([ADR 0191](../../adr/0191-the-step-a-findings-route-goes-back-to-records-a-disposition-for-each-finding.md), [ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)).
The brewer the route brings back may record in its out doc's `dispositions` what it did with each finding of its route input: `fixed`, `dismissed` with a `why`, or `pre-existing` with a `why` ([out doc](out-doc.md)); the default Cellarman's prompt says to.
A finding the brewer cannot settle, because it needs a decision it cannot make from the order, the code or a decision record, ends its attempt with its questions (`needs_input`), as the default Cellarman's prompt says ([ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)).
A route back from a child of a parallel group also lists, after that child's out doc, the findings of the group's other children in the same round, each under its step, its lenses and its verdict (`asks for changes`, `approves with findings`, or `approves` with no findings), those that asked for changes first ([ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)); a blocked child's are the ones `step.finished` recorded, a succeeded one's its out doc's; past 64 KiB those lines are cut first, never the routing child's out doc, and a last line counts what each child left out ([ADR 0349](../../adr/0349-a-group-s-round-back-carries-every-finding-of-the-round.md)).
A disposition of a finding listed under another step names that `step`; a round of a step reads the dispositions that name it, or that name no step when the route was its own, and another step's route answers only a round that tasted since the brewer it brought back last ran, so another child's next round reads what the Cellarman did with its findings ([ADR 0349](../../adr/0349-a-group-s-round-back-carries-every-finding-of-the-round.md)).
A later round of a Taster's step, one after an earlier attempt of it that reviewed, gets the input `earlierFindings`: each earlier round's findings in the order the rounds ran, a success's from its event and a blocked round's from the out doc in its worktree as the route input reads it, each with the disposition at its place (file and line as the Taster wrote them) of its step from the attempt a findings route brought back after that round, its own or another child's of its group, or a retry or an answered attempt that continued it, or `open`; a round whose out doc cannot be read says so, held to 64 KiB.
The engine's fixed text then says never to raise a fixed, dismissed or pre-existing finding again as it was, and to raise a fix that did not fix one, or a dismissal held wrong, once as a new finding that names the old one and says why.
It also says to check the evidence a dismissed or pre-existing finding's `why` names, and to hold one that names none, or whose evidence does not show what it claims, as wrong ([ADR 0204](../../adr/0204-a-dismissal-names-the-evidence-that-shows-it.md)).
The pull request lists the `pre-existing` ones ([open the pull request](open-pull-request.md)), and once every pull request of the batch merged, the driver opens a Brewery issue for each one paired with a Taster's finding ([ADR 0356](../../adr/0356-a-pre-existing-finding-becomes-a-brewery-issue-once-the-pull-request-merges.md)).
A findings blocker on a step with no route is Stuck as any other blocker, with its summary only.
A `lens` or `lenses` on a brewer that is no Taster, a Taster's step with neither, and a lens no folder has fail the load with a line each.
Brett proposes mutants as one-line structured edits ([ADR 0034](../../adr/0034-brett-mutants-as-structured-line-edits.md)); the engine's run of them confirms which ones the tests miss ([ADR 0313](../../adr/0313-the-engine-runs-brett-s-mutants-in-scratch-trees-against-a-fresh-baseline.md)), counting only a test the mutant makes fail ([ADR 0040](../../adr/0040-brett-covers-scripts-and-kills-need-a-failing-test.md)), and these findings are advisory by default unless the step blocks ([ADR 0319](../../adr/0319-brett-s-survivors-are-listed-where-findings-are-and-a-blocking-brett-step-sends-them-back.md)).

Lenses live in two folders, by what they review ([ADR 0143](../../adr/0143-lenses-in-two-folders-diff-and-plan.md)): `lenses/diff/` for a diff (code) and `lenses/plan/` for a plan (an order's breakdown, #391), in the Brewery and in `defaults/lenses/`.
A lens is named by its folder and file name, `diff/security` or `plan/coverage`, so the two folders can hold a lens of the same file name.
Default **tasting lenses** (`defaults/lenses/diff/*.md`): correctness, security, cross-platform, design conformance, test design, necessity, acceptance; `defaults/lenses/plan/` has seven ([ADR 0321](../../adr/0321-mash-and-decoction-review-a-plan-through-the-plan-lenses-apart.md)): [coverage](../../../defaults/lenses/plan/coverage.md), [sizing](../../../defaults/lenses/plan/sizing.md), [naive reader](../../../defaults/lenses/plan/naive-reader.md) ([ADR 0312](../../adr/0312-a-taster-on-a-plan-lens-step-reads-the-batch-s-latest-plan-and-ties-each-finding-to-a-sub-order.md)), [order and overlap](../../../defaults/lenses/plan/order-and-overlap.md), [testability](../../../defaults/lenses/plan/testability.md), [recipe fit](../../../defaults/lenses/plan/recipe-fit.md) and [security](../../../defaults/lenses/plan/security.md); what each reports is in its file.
The security plan lens also reads `.foedrum/trust-boundaries.md` in each repository a sub-order builds in, when a person wrote one: it adds to the lens's question and never limits it.
Acceptance reads the order and says of each item of its "What to build" checklist whether the change built it, built part of it or left it out, each with the line that shows it, and names what the change builds unasked; its follow-ups are proposals in its out doc, never issues it files.
The order holds its recorded scope decisions, which acceptance judges the items by and necessity reads for recorded proof: the comments on the issue its order holds, in a batch ([ADR 0333](../../adr/0333-a-brewer-s-order-holds-the-maintainers-comments.md), on a claimed target issue every one its approval approved, [ADR 0373](../../adr/0373-a-label-approved-order-s-brewers-read-every-comment-made-before-its-approval.md)), and its maintainers' in `/brew-review`, and in `/brew-review` also the pull request's description ([ADR 0276](../../adr/0276-the-order-a-review-reads-holds-its-recorded-scope-decisions.md)); what each lens reports is in its file in `defaults/lenses/diff/`.
A sub-order's Tasters also get its entry of the approved plan, which only [acceptance](../../../defaults/lenses/diff/acceptance.md) reads as a scope record ([ADR 0290](../../adr/0290-a-sub-order-s-brewers-read-its-plan-entry-from-the-parent-s-recorded-plan.md)).
A sub-order's entry may hold a `Security` line, the Brewmaster's own mark of what outside input it lets in and what that reaches: [security](../../../defaults/lenses/diff/security.md) checks what it names first, and an entry without one never means safe ([ADR 0322](../../adr/0322-a-sub-order-s-security-mark-is-the-brewmaster-s-and-reaches-its-plan-entry.md)).
In `deep-mash`, the only default recipe that runs it, the [security plan lens](../../../defaults/lenses/plan/security.md)'s findings go back to the Brewmaster with the group's other findings ([ADR 0321](../../adr/0321-mash-and-decoction-review-a-plan-through-the-plan-lenses-apart.md), [ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)), and what the rounds leave reaches a person at gate 1; a person corrects a missed mark with `foedrum lauter` ([ADR 0322](../../adr/0322-a-sub-order-s-security-mark-is-the-brewmaster-s-and-reaches-its-plan-entry.md)).
A release-blocked sub-order's Tasters also get the release it waited for, whose bump [acceptance](../../../defaults/lenses/diff/acceptance.md) judges as part of what the change builds ([ADR 0291](../../adr/0291-a-release-blocked-sub-order-s-brewers-read-the-release-it-waited-for-as-written.md)).
A Brewery adds or overrides lenses by committing files to `lenses/diff/` or `lenses/plan/`: a file of the same folder and name replaces the default, and a brewer input `"from": "lens:diff/<name>"` or `"lens:plan/<name>"` takes the lens's text ([ADR 0098](../../adr/0098-foedrum-s-defaults-load-under-the-brewery-s-files-and-a-brewer-input-can-name-a-lens.md)).
A step with a `lens` reviews a diff once a step before it (in the order the recipe lists its steps, nested ones included) produces commits, and a plan otherwise; a lens of the other folder fails the load with a line that names the step and the lens, and a lens file directly in `lenses/` fails it with a line that says where it belongs.
A step whose lenses review a diff gets the change; a step whose lenses review a plan gets, after its brewer's own inputs, the batch's latest plan as `plan` (the out doc of the latest attempt that succeeded with a plan, read where it ran, its tag naming that attempt), the Brewery's repositories as `repositories`, and no change ([ADR 0312](../../adr/0312-a-taster-on-a-plan-lens-step-reads-the-batch-s-latest-plan-and-ties-each-finding-to-a-sub-order.md)), and the Brewery's recipes of type `brew` as `recipes`, each with its type, description and guidance ([ADR 0375](../../adr/0375-every-recipe-offered-as-a-choice-carries-guidance-the-models-that-choose-read.md)), the list the Brewmaster reads ([ADR 0321](../../adr/0321-mash-and-decoction-review-a-plan-through-the-plan-lenses-apart.md), [ADR 0365](../../adr/0365-a-recipe-has-a-type-mash-or-brew.md)).
Such a step before the batch has a plan fails its attempt's plan, and its house yeast is read at the commit its worktree holds, with no file changed.
A plan's Taster ties a finding about one sub-order to it by starting its `why` with the sub-order's id, and leaves `file` and `line` out unless it points at a file and line it read in a repository; `tasting` has no field for it.
A plan lens step's findings route goes back to the plan step as any findings route does; its routed attempt gets, besides the route input, the batch's latest plan as the `plan` input its brewer names, as a step a person chose does ([ADR 0286](../../adr/0286-foedrum-lauter-corrects-a-plan-at-gate-1-in-an-interactive-session.md), [ADR 0318](../../adr/0318-a-plan-lens-s-findings-go-back-to-the-plan-step-and-the-plan-comment-lists-the-review-notes-left.md)).
The default [Brewmaster](../../../defaults/brewers/brewmaster.md) corrects the plan and records a `fixed` or `dismissed` disposition per finding.
A later round of a plan lens step reads a finding with no file as about the plan as a whole, and the engine's fixed text says that a dismissal names a line of the order, a file and line in a repository, or a field of the plan ([ADR 0318](../../adr/0318-a-plan-lens-s-findings-go-back-to-the-plan-step-and-the-plan-comment-lists-the-review-notes-left.md)).
What the rounds leave at gate 1 is listed in the plan comment's Review section ([GitHub surfaces](../humans/github.md)).
When the rounds run out with findings still open, a plan's batch is Stuck as any other, and the plan reaches its review after a person's `skip` ([ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)).
A group's children share its route's `maxRounds`, whichever child's findings sent the batch back, so `mash`'s and `deep-mash`'s lenses send a plan back twice at most in each run of the recipe ([ADR 0369](../../adr/0369-a-recipe-s-routes-count-their-rounds-per-run-an-open-pull-request-s-over-the-batch-s-life.md)), each round running again only the lenses that had findings ([ADR 0357](../../adr/0357-after-a-round-back-a-group-runs-again-only-its-every-round-children-and-those-with-findings.md)), and every child's findings go back in the same round ([ADR 0349](../../adr/0349-a-group-s-round-back-carries-every-finding-of-the-round.md), [ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)); a lens step, of a plan or of a diff, counts its attempts per round as every brewer step does, so each round, the plan step's too, has its brewer's attempts afresh ([brewers](brewers.md), [ADR 0325](../../adr/0325-a-step-counts-its-attempts-per-round.md)).
Test-first and lenses: [ADR 0017](../../adr/0017-test-first-recipes-and-tasting-lenses.md); the fifth lens: [ADR 0022](../../adr/0022-five-default-lenses.md); the sixth: [ADR 0173](../../adr/0173-necessity-ships-as-a-default-diff-lens.md); the seventh: [ADR 0197](../../adr/0197-acceptance-ships-as-a-default-diff-lens-and-nip-tastes-through-it-beside-correctness.md).

**Parallel groups.** A `parallel` step's children are Tasters' lens steps without `when`, and a child whose brewer is no Taster, or produces commits, fails the load, so no child writes the task tree ([ADR 0168](../../adr/0168-a-parallel-group-runs-its-lens-steps-side-by-side.md)).
Each child is a step of its own, with its own attempts, lease, worktree, deadline and stall limit, and the status comment lists the children where the group stands.
Entering the group schedules every child at once, except after its own round back for findings: then it schedules each child with `everyRound: true` and each whose latest attempt did not approve, and a child that approved with no finding keeps that approval and counts as ended, in every later round too; a person's recipe choice or rewind runs it whole again ([ADR 0357](../../adr/0357-after-a-round-back-a-group-runs-again-only-its-every-round-children-and-those-with-findings.md)).
A failed child is tried again on its own, and a child that makes the batch Stuck for another reason does so at once, abandoning the children still in flight.
A child ends when its latest attempt succeeded, or was blocked by findings, whatever rounds are left; the group ends when every child ended ([ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)).
Then, when any child was blocked by findings, the batch goes back once along the group's `onFindings` route, recorded as from the first such child in the recipe's order; once the group's rounds are used it is Stuck instead, with every such child's findings of the round open, the first child's step and summary on the reason; otherwise the group's verdict is `approve` and the batch goes on.
A route counts its rounds from the attempts of its `goto` step that any child of the group sent back, so a log written while each child had a route of its own counts every child's rounds as the group's.
A partial pass is a round like a whole one, and a kept child's step line and pull request report say the round its approval is from.
A `retry` or `answer` of a child also schedules every child of its group that has not ended, a `skip` of a child skips the rest of its group, and a `rewind` to a child runs the whole group again; a rewind or a route back to the group itself is refused, and so is a child's route back to a sibling.
The batch leaves a group only while no attempt of it runs.
A group whose recipe file gained a child while the batch was in it makes the batch Stuck on a divergence, naming that child.
A kill mid-group retries only the children that had not ended: the supervisor loses one attempt of a batch per tick and keeps the expired leases of the others for the ticks after.
A child may be a step of several lenses together, beside lens steps apart.

## Lenses together

A Taster's step may name `lenses` instead of `lens`: two or more lenses of one folder, each once, that one Taster reviews through together ([ADR 0220](../../adr/0220-a-taster-s-step-can-run-several-lenses-together.md)).
The schema refuses `lenses` beside `lens`, with one lens or with a lens named twice; the load refuses it where it refuses `lens`, names each lens no folder has, and checks each against what the step reviews.
It is one step, with one attempt at a time, one lease, deadline, out doc and status comment line, so a retry, a skip, a rewind, a stall or a kill takes every lens of it at once.
Its turns and stall limit are its brewer's, and its model and effort too unless the step names them; a recipe that wants one lens on another model runs it apart, on a step of its own that names another alias ([ADR 0321](../../adr/0321-mash-and-decoction-review-a-plan-through-the-plan-lenses-apart.md), [ADR 0352](../../adr/0352-nip-s-correctness-acceptance-and-security-tasters-run-at-high-effort-without-the-rules-read.md)).
Its Taster gets the change once, and the lenses as its one input `lens`: every lens file in the step's order, under a source that names each lens and its file.
The engine adds a fixed text, only on such a step: review through each lens in turn, give every finding the `lens` it was found through, read a lens file's "stay inside this one" as "report under this lens only what it asks", and start each `riskLeft` item with its lens; a one-lens prompt stays as it is.
On plan lenses that text says to review the plan, and its example finding has no `file` or `line` and a `why` that starts with the sub-order's id ([ADR 0323](../../adr/0323-a-seeded-plan-measures-the-plan-lenses-apart-and-together-after-the-merge.md)).
Its house yeast is the rules each of its lenses would get apart, each once, in the order of its lenses.
Each finding of its out doc names one of the step's lenses, or the out doc is invalid and the attempt fails, as on any schema error; on a one-lens step a finding may leave its lens out, and one that names a lens names the step's.
A finding without a `lens` is its step's lens's: `lensOfFinding` gives that to a reader that counts by lens (#561).
The out doc has one `verdict`, from the findings of every lens, and any lens's finding sends the step's findings back.
A findings route from it carries its whole out doc, every lens's findings at once; a group of lenses apart carries the routing child's out doc and the other children's findings of the round ([ADR 0349](../../adr/0349-a-group-s-round-back-carries-every-finding-of-the-round.md)).
Its findings show their lens before their severity (`diff/security, major in src/a.ts:3`) in a later round's `earlierFindings`, the status comment, the pull request body and the Stuck reason.
How the status comment lists them, per step or per lens, is [GitHub surfaces](../humans/github.md)'s.
Nip, Mash and Deep-mash run their lenses apart: together was measured on a seeded change and fell short of the rule that picks it ([ADR 0224](../../adr/0224-the-default-recipes-taste-apart-or-together-as-a-seeded-comparison-measured.md)).
Deep-brew runs all seven diff lenses together in one Taster on the `planner` alias at the Taster's own effort, the setup in which three lenses together found the main defect of the real comparison in 3 of 3 runs ([ADR 0347](../../adr/0347-tasting-compare-real-measures-tasters-without-the-rules-read-and-three-lenses-together.md), [ADR 0366](../../adr/0366-deep-brew-builds-a-change-that-can-reach-a-lot-through-every-diff-lens-and-a-blocking-brett-step.md)).
Every setup's Tasters send their findings back to its scripted step, once, as every Taster's go back; that step's next turn ends blocked with the summary `tasting-compare ends the run after its first tasting round`, and a run's record reads that Stuck as `after round 1`, no failure, so a run measures round 1 alone, a Taster blocked by its findings counted as a review with the findings its event recorded ([ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)).

**Brett's mutants are one-line edits, checked against the change.** Brett ([`defaults/brewers/brett.md`](../../../defaults/brewers/brett.md)) proposes a few mutants in a `mutants` out doc, at most 5, each as a one-line structured edit (`file`, `line`, `original`, `replacement`, `description`), never a patch ([ADR 0034](../../adr/0034-brett-mutants-as-structured-line-edits.md)); a `done` with none is valid, its body saying there was nothing worth mutating ([ADR 0311](../../adr/0311-brett-s-mutants-of-a-target-land-on-lines-the-change-added-in-a-code-area-of-the-test-map.md)).
A brewer whose out doc schema is `mutants` gets the input `change`, as a Finer and a Taster that reviews a diff do.
`mutantCheck` checks each edit on its own and gives the reason for one it refuses (in `src/checks/mutants.ts`, [ADR 0311](../../adr/0311-brett-s-mutants-of-a-target-land-on-lines-the-change-added-in-a-code-area-of-the-test-map.md)): a plain relative path, in a code area of the effective [test map](../checks/test-map.md), on a line whose text is `original` and that the batch's change added, and a replacement of one line that changes more than whitespace and adds no word or quoted string the original lacks.
Comments are left out of that comparison only for JavaScript, TypeScript and the C family, by the file's extension; elsewhere a comment counts as the line's text.
Where they are left out, a replacement that changes only comments changes nothing, as one that changes only whitespace does ([ADR 0314](../../adr/0314-a-brett-mutant-that-changes-only-a-comment-changes-nothing.md)).
In JavaScript and TypeScript a template literal is not one string: its text takes a word of the line's code or a literal's text, not of a comment or a plain string, its `${}` only names of the line's code, and a replacement that leaves it open or closes it is refused; a line the check cannot read with confidence, such as a slash before a backtick, is held to the word rule above as well ([ADR 0341](../../adr/0341-a-mutant-can-land-inside-a-template-literal.md)).
Foedrum's own review tooling checks the same replacement rule for files under `src/` and `scripts/` of this repository ([ADR 0040](../../adr/0040-brett-covers-scripts-and-kills-need-a-failing-test.md)).
**The engine runs the mutants once Brett's session succeeded** (`mutated`, `src/checks/mutant-run.ts`, [ADR 0313](../../adr/0313-the-engine-runs-brett-s-mutants-in-scratch-trees-against-a-fresh-baseline.md)): it checks each against the batch's change, from where the batch started to the attempt's commit, then runs the repository's whole `test` check, its setup first, once in a scratch tree of that commit as the baseline and once per valid mutant in a fresh scratch tree with the mutant's file written over the commit's, one at a time.
An invalid mutant never runs, and when none is valid, not even the baseline does.
Every run ends a minute before the step's deadline; a mutant's run gets three times the baseline's time, at least a minute.
Each verdict goes on the attempt's `step.finished` as `mutants`: `killed` with the first test that fails because of it (it failed more often or passed less often than at the baseline, or no longer reports), `survived`, `timedOut` (killed), `invalid` with the reason, or `notJudged` with why (no passing test of the mutant's module at the baseline, no report, a mutant that broke the build, a run that did not exit, or the deadline), and `foedrum replay` lists them.
A survivor's record also keeps its edit, the line it replaced and the line that replaced it, cut and filtered as its other texts are ([ADR 0319](../../adr/0319-brett-s-survivors-are-listed-where-findings-are-and-a-blocking-brett-step-sends-them-back.md)).
The runs go at normal priority; running them below normal is #809.
Only a test that fails because of the mutant catches it: the reading is compared test by test with the reading of the unmutated tree, so a test that fails anyway, a time budget or a coverage threshold does not count ([ADR 0040](../../adr/0040-brett-covers-scripts-and-kills-need-a-failing-test.md)).
Only mutants the tests do not catch are **confirmed survivors**.
When the work around the session throws, every proposed mutant is recorded `notJudged` with why, and the attempt keeps its own end ([ADR 0319](../../adr/0319-brett-s-survivors-are-listed-where-findings-are-and-a-blocking-brett-step-sends-them-back.md)).
**The survivors are listed where findings are** (`src/engine/survivors.ts`, [ADR 0319](../../adr/0319-brett-s-survivors-are-listed-where-findings-are-and-a-blocking-brett-step-sends-them-back.md)): for a Brett step whose latest attempt succeeded, the status comment's Findings and the pull request's report of the step list each survivor on one line, `survived: <file>:<line>: - <original> / + <replacement>: <description>`, and count the mutants by verdict (`2 mutants: 1 survived, 1 killed`); the merge brief's Risk left lists each survivor.
Every text is model or repository text, told through the same filters as a Taster's findings.
A Brett step is advisory by default: its attempt succeeds whatever the verdicts, and a survivor is listed, never a finding.
With `"blocking": true`, an attempt with a survivor ends blocked by findings, each survivor a `major` finding on its file and line, introduced, whose `why` holds the edit and the description; its `onBlocked` findings route sends the batch back with them, as a Taster's `onFindings` route does with its findings ([ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)), the blocked reason names each survivor, and an attempt with no survivor succeeds: a later round whose mutants all die, one with none, and one whose mutants were all invalid or not judged, whose count line says so.
Deep-brew runs a blocking Brett step after green and before its Taster, whose survivors go back to the Gauger, twice at most in each run; the Gauger's round starts at its own latest success, without the code, and its prompt has it add a test per survivor ([ADR 0358](../../adr/0358-an-older-test-changes-only-in-the-gauger-s-red-commit.md), [ADR 0366](../../adr/0366-deep-brew-builds-a-change-that-can-reach-a-lot-through-every-diff-lens-and-a-blocking-brett-step.md)).
No other default recipe runs Brett: a Brewery opts in with a step `{ "id": "mutants", "brewer": "brett" }` in its own recipe, after the change's tests run, and `npm run demo`'s recipe has one after its taste group, since a parallel group runs only Tasters ([ADR 0168](../../adr/0168-a-parallel-group-runs-its-lens-steps-side-by-side.md), [ADR 0319](../../adr/0319-brett-s-survivors-are-listed-where-findings-are-and-a-blocking-brett-step-sends-them-back.md)).
