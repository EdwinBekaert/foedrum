# Defaults

## Default recipes

Mash-in, Mash and Decoction are of type `mash`, Nip and Deep-brew of type `brew` ([ADR 0365](../../adr/0365-a-recipe-has-a-type-mash-or-brew.md)).

| Recipe | Use |
|---|---|
| Mash-in | the first step of a mash: the Innkeeper suggests one of the Brewery's other recipes, each listed with its type, and asks which brews the order; the answer chooses it; `foedrum mash` routes to it, nobody labels it; shipped in `defaults/recipes/mash-in.jsonc` ([ADR 0232](../../adr/0232-foedrum-mash-routes-an-issue-to-the-innkeeper-whose-question-the-answer-chooses-the-recipe-by.md)) |
| Nip | quick fix, test-first: the Maltster's survey of the test map and its check against one run, neither of which runs anything when the map is fresh ([ADR 0298](../../adr/0298-the-maltster-s-survey-ends-without-a-session-for-a-fresh-map-and-its-check-sends-a-disagreement-back-once.md)), the Gauger, a red proof, Cellarman, the Finer ([ADR 0166](../../adr/0166-the-finer-simplifies-a-batch-s-change-before-green.md)), the green checks (`build`, `typecheck`, `lint`, `format`, `test`; [ADR 0157](../../adr/0157-nip-s-green-step-runs-the-typecheck.md)) on the task tree with the base merged in ([ADR 0164](../../adr/0164-a-batch-merges-its-base-before-its-last-check.md)), then four Tasters side by side, through `diff/correctness`, `diff/acceptance` ([ADR 0197](../../adr/0197-acceptance-ships-as-a-default-diff-lens-and-nip-tastes-through-it-beside-correctness.md)), `diff/security` ([ADR 0306](../../adr/0306-nip-tastes-through-the-security-lens-beside-correctness-and-acceptance.md)) and `diff/design-conformance` ([ADR 0343](../../adr/0343-nip-tastes-through-design-conformance-as-a-fourth-advisory-lens.md)), every finding of the four sending the batch back to Cellarman, twice at most in all ([ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)), after which correctness and the Tasters that had findings run again ([ADR 0357](../../adr/0357-after-a-round-back-a-group-runs-again-only-its-every-round-children-and-those-with-findings.md)), and the pull request; shipped in `defaults/recipes/nip.jsonc` with the default Maltster, Gauger, Cellarman, Finer and Taster, which name the `builder` alias ([ADR 0100](../../adr/0100-nip-ships-with-a-default-cellarman-and-the-default-brewers-name-the-builder-alias.md)) |
| Mash | a plan of an order: the Brewmaster breaks it into sub-orders, one repository each; three Tasters review it side by side, through `plan/coverage` on the `planner` alias, `plan/sizing` and `plan/naive-reader`, every finding sending the plan back to the Brewmaster, twice at most in all ([ADR 0321](../../adr/0321-mash-and-decoction-review-a-plan-through-the-plan-lenses-apart.md), [ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)); it is shown as the plan comment on its issue, and the batch waits Stuck with its plan ready for review, nothing built ([ADR 0275](../../adr/0275-a-finished-plan-waits-stuck-for-its-review-and-every-comment-links-the-doc-it-sums-up.md)); its `lauter` step, `chosenBy` lauter, runs only when a person chooses `foedrum lauter` there: the Brewmaster corrects the plan with them in a session ([ADR 0286](../../adr/0286-foedrum-lauter-corrects-a-plan-at-gate-1-in-an-interactive-session.md)); shipped in `defaults/recipes/mash.jsonc` ([ADR 0234](../../adr/0234-the-brewmaster-writes-a-plan-on-the-planner-alias-and-mash-runs-it.md)) |
| Decoction | a careful plan of a large order: Mash's steps with all seven plan lenses in its group, also `plan/order-and-overlap`, `plan/testability`, `plan/recipe-fit`, and `plan/security`, which runs on the `planner` alias as `plan/coverage` does; shipped in `defaults/recipes/decoction.jsonc` ([ADR 0321](../../adr/0321-mash-and-decoction-review-a-plan-through-the-plan-lenses-apart.md)) |
| Deep-brew | a change that can reach a lot, whatever its size, test-first: Nip's steps from the survey to green, then a blocking Brett step whose surviving mutants go back to the Gauger, twice at most, who adds the tests that catch them before the Cellarman writes the code again ([ADR 0319](../../adr/0319-brett-s-survivors-are-listed-where-findings-are-and-a-blocking-brett-step-sends-them-back.md), [ADR 0358](../../adr/0358-an-older-test-changes-only-in-the-gauger-s-red-commit.md)), then one Taster on the `planner` alias through all seven diff lenses together ([ADR 0220](../../adr/0220-a-taster-s-step-can-run-several-lenses-together.md)), every finding sending the batch back to Cellarman, twice at most ([ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)), and the pull request; the build stage of the full recipe, chosen by what a change can reach, not by its size; shipped in `defaults/recipes/deep-brew.jsonc` ([ADR 0366](../../adr/0366-deep-brew-builds-a-change-that-can-reach-a-lot-through-every-diff-lens-and-a-blocking-brett-step.md)) |
| Keg | multi-repository change with release dependencies between repositories |
| Pilot batch | spike / research, no PR |
| Infection check | triage of target-repository issues: reproduce, root cause, findings as Brewery orders ([target-repository issues](../intake/target-issues.md)) |
| Guest tasting | review someone else's pull request |
| Re-pitch | re-review a pull request after fixes |
| Refresh | dependency upgrades (scheduled) |

## Default brewers

Default models are aliases in `models.jsonc`, resolved to the current model of that family; blind tasting may propose changes.

| Brewer | Role | Model | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Innkeeper | Reads an order and asks which recipe brews it, with a suggestion and the reason (read-only; [ADR 0232](../../adr/0232-foedrum-mash-routes-an-issue-to-the-innkeeper-whose-question-the-answer-chooses-the-recipe-by.md)) | builder (sonnet) | low |
| Maltster | Proposes a repository's test map from its manifests when it is missing or stale, in the test-first recipes' `survey` step, committing nothing else ([ADR 0298](../../adr/0298-the-maltster-s-survey-ends-without-a-session-for-a-fresh-map-and-its-check-sends-a-disagreement-back-once.md)); in `mash` the Brewmaster gathers its own context (ADR 0234) | builder (sonnet) | medium |
| Brewmaster | Gathers the context and writes the plan: sub-orders with what each is done when, read-only | planner (opus) | high |
| Gauger | Writes failing tests (and minimal stubs, no implementation) from the order and, for a sub-order, its plan entry ([ADR 0290](../../adr/0290-a-sub-order-s-brewers-read-its-plan-entry-from-the-parent-s-recorded-plan.md)), and updates the older tests the change breaks ([ADR 0358](../../adr/0358-an-older-test-changes-only-in-the-gauger-s-red-commit.md)) | builder (sonnet) | high |
| Cellarman | Reads the Gauger's tests, then makes them pass without changing them or a test file the base holds ([ADR 0358](../../adr/0358-an-older-test-changes-only-in-the-gauger-s-red-commit.md)); a sub-order's Cellarman also reads its plan entry, its security mark included ([ADR 0290](../../adr/0290-a-sub-order-s-brewers-read-its-plan-entry-from-the-parent-s-recorded-plan.md), [ADR 0322](../../adr/0322-a-sub-order-s-security-mark-is-the-brewmaster-s-and-reaches-its-plan-entry.md)); a fix round reads its earlier sections and returns them for the whole change ([ADR 0353](../../adr/0353-a-fix-round-s-cellarman-reads-its-earlier-sections-and-returns-them-for-the-whole-change.md)) | builder (sonnet) | high |
| Finer | Simplifies the change without changing what it does, and commits it ([fining](fining.md)) | builder (sonnet) | high |
| Taster | Reviews a diff or a plan through its step's lens, or several together (read-only; [tasting](tasting.md)) | builder (sonnet); planner (opus) on the steps of `plan/coverage` and `plan/security`, and on Deep-brew's `taste` | medium |
| Brett | Proposes up to 5 mutants of the change, each a one-line edit of a line it added, which the engine checks (read-only; [tasting](tasting.md), [ADR 0311](../../adr/0311-brett-s-mutants-of-a-target-land-on-lines-the-change-added-in-a-code-area-of-the-test-map.md)); Deep-brew runs it blocking, before its Taster ([ADR 0366](../../adr/0366-deep-brew-builds-a-change-that-can-reach-a-lot-through-every-diff-lens-and-a-blocking-brett-step.md)); the engine runs them in scratch trees and records each verdict ([ADR 0313](../../adr/0313-the-engine-runs-brett-s-mutants-in-scratch-trees-against-a-fresh-baseline.md)); the survivors are listed in the status comment, the pull request and the merge brief, and block a step with `"blocking": true` ([ADR 0319](../../adr/0319-brett-s-survivors-are-listed-where-findings-are-and-a-blocking-brett-step-sends-them-back.md)) | builder (sonnet) | medium |
| Cooper | Fixes review findings | sonnet | high |
| Blender | Resolves merge conflicts (bounded) | opus | medium |
| Microbiologist | Bug triage: reproduce, root cause, failing test | opus | medium |
| Lab tech | Verifies a fix in a real environment | sonnet | high |
| Tasting panel | Runs evaluations | — (uses the model under test) | — |

Shipped in `defaults/brewers/` so far: the Gauger, whose test rules are the test-design lens, passed in as `"from": "lens:diff/test-design"`, the Maltster, which proposes a repository's test map as its out doc (`survey`) and commits only `.foedrum/test-map.jsonc` ([ADR 0282](../../adr/0282-a-stale-test-map-sends-back-the-change-that-made-it-and-the-maltster-surveys-a-new-one.md), [ADR 0298](../../adr/0298-the-maltster-s-survey-ends-without-a-session-for-a-fresh-map-and-its-check-sends-a-disagreement-back-once.md)), Cellarman, which also writes the pull request's sections (its out doc schema `pullRequest`, [ADR 0167](../../adr/0167-a-brewed-pull-request-follows-one-layout-and-opens-as-a-draft-unless-every-taster-approves.md)), the Finer, which simplifies the change before green ([ADR 0166](../../adr/0166-the-finer-simplifies-a-batch-s-change-before-green.md)), and the Taster, which reviews through its step's lens and answers with findings and a verdict ([ADR 0161](../../adr/0161-lens-steps-run-a-taster-whose-findings-are-advisory-unless-the-step-blocks.md)), the Innkeeper, which asks with the out doc schema `recipeChoice` among the recipes the engine fills its `"from": "recipes"` input with ([ADR 0232](../../adr/0232-foedrum-mash-routes-an-issue-to-the-innkeeper-whose-question-the-answer-chooses-the-recipe-by.md)), reading the repositories of its `"from": "repositories"` input the order names, each in its checkout's folder in a Brewery order's folder of checkouts ([ADR 0301](../../adr/0301-a-brewery-order-s-read-only-attempt-reads-every-repository-in-a-checkout-of-each.md)), Brett, read-only, which proposes up to 5 mutants of the change as its out doc (`mutants`, [ADR 0311](../../adr/0311-brett-s-mutants-of-a-target-land-on-lines-the-change-added-in-a-code-area-of-the-test-map.md)), all on the alias `builder`, and the Brewmaster, which writes an order's plan as its out doc (`plan`, [ADR 0233](../../adr/0233-a-plan-is-an-out-doc-whose-paths-the-engine-checks-shown-as-one-comment-per-order.md)) from the order and the Brewery's repositories, on the alias `planner` ([ADR 0234](../../adr/0234-the-brewmaster-writes-a-plan-on-the-planner-alias-and-mash-runs-it.md)); Foedrum's `defaults/models.jsonc` maps `builder` to `sonnet` and `planner` to `opus`, and a Brewery's `models.jsonc` may map either to another model ([ADR 0153](../../adr/0153-foedrum-s-defaults-map-their-own-model-aliases.md), [ADR 0100](../../adr/0100-nip-ships-with-a-default-cellarman-and-the-default-brewers-name-the-builder-alias.md)).
Nip fines its change after Cellarman and, after its green checks, tastes through `diff/correctness`, `diff/acceptance`, `diff/security` and `diff/design-conformance` in a parallel group named `taste`: every finding of the four sends the batch back to Cellarman, through the Finer and green again, twice at most in all before the batch is Stuck with the findings still open ([ADR 0264](../../adr/0264-nip-s-correctness-taster-blocks-and-sends-its-findings-back-to-cellarman.md), [ADR 0306](../../adr/0306-nip-tastes-through-the-security-lens-beside-correctness-and-acceptance.md), [ADR 0343](../../adr/0343-nip-tastes-through-design-conformance-as-a-fourth-advisory-lens.md), [ADR 0354](../../adr/0354-every-finding-of-a-taster-goes-back-along-one-on-findings-route.md)).
Its correctness, acceptance and security steps run at high effort, and design conformance at the Taster's own medium ([ADR 0352](../../adr/0352-nip-s-correctness-acceptance-and-security-tasters-run-at-high-effort-without-the-rules-read.md)).
The comparison of #879 measured design conformance at about 300k more tokens per tasting round, on three heads of one repository, and it missed that issue's bar ([ADR 0343](../../adr/0343-nip-tastes-through-design-conformance-as-a-fourth-advisory-lens.md)).
No default lens reports simplifications: a Taster after the Finer would review what the Finer did.
The others follow as the engine learns what they need.

Default lenses: [tasting](tasting.md).
