# On GitHub

On GitHub, for the autonomous driver and asynchronous teammates:

- One **status comment** per order, edited in place: recipe (as a Mermaid diagram with the current step highlighted), steps, state, assumptions brewers made, and under **Receipts** one line per attempt with its duration, turns, tokens and denials, at most 30, and never a cost ([ADR 0181](../../adr/0181-receipts-show-no-cost.md)).
  In M1 every batch of a Brewery issue has one, a claimed issue's on its Brewery issue (below), without the diagram: the batch and its lane (Stuck with its reason and options, and the open findings and a step-back when a findings route ran out of rounds, [tasting](../recipes/tasting.md); Conditioning with what it waits for; and, while a person's pause holds it, Paused with who, from where, why and the `--by` instant, [pause and continue](pause.md)), the recipe, its path (the attempts in the order they started, each marked, on one line, [ADR 0154](../../adr/0154-lane-labels-on-the-order-s-issue-and-the-status-comment-s-path-line.md); a failed attempt with its reason's first clause, and an answered question with its latest answer, who gave it and from where, on the path and on the step line of a step whose latest attempt it answered, [ADR 0261](../../adr/0261-the-status-path-says-why-a-failed-attempt-failed-and-what-answered-a-question.md)) and its steps with the current one marked, the sensitive files the latest push of its pull request recorded, from then on, as [configuration](../recipes/configuration.md) says (ADR 0201), the assumptions from the out docs, a plan step's ending with a link to its out doc (below), a Taster's findings while its step's latest attempt succeeded, ten per step and a count of the rest ([ADR 0161](../../adr/0161-lens-steps-run-a-taster-whose-findings-are-advisory-unless-the-step-blocks.md)), or, when every finding of the step names its lens, as on a step of several lenses together, ten per lens, each after its lens ([ADR 0220](../../adr/0220-a-taster-s-step-can-run-several-lenses-together.md)), and its risk left under Risk left, five per step and a count of the rest ([ADR 0214](../../adr/0214-a-taster-s-out-doc-says-what-risk-is-left.md)), the new tests and the regression tests a step's latest success named, ten of each per step and a count of the rest ([ADR 0170](../../adr/0170-the-red-proof-accepts-the-regression-tests-a-gauger-names.md)), a Finer's changes or why it had none, as its findings are ([ADR 0166](../../adr/0166-the-finer-simplifies-a-batch-s-change-before-green.md)), each attempt's receipt under Receipts ([ADR 0172](../../adr/0172-an-attempt-s-receipt-from-its-stream.md)), the step line of a running attempt and the receipt line of an attempt that resumed after a turn overrun ending with `resumed after the turn budget` ([ADR 0175](../../adr/0175-a-turn-overrun-is-retried-by-resuming-its-session-and-keeps-its-out-doc.md)), and the pull requests once on tap, each with its latest base check, the other open pull requests its head conflicts with ([ADR 0230](../../adr/0230-open-pull-requests-of-one-repository-are-merge-tree-checked-against-each-other.md)) and the checks on its head as [open pull requests](../intake/open-pull-requests.md) says, and a line when the latest round left its body as it is, edited or with no marker ([ADR 0227](../../adr/0227-a-later-round-renders-the-pull-request-s-body-again-unless-someone-edited-it.md)), every text a brewer wrote escaped to one line, and left out for a fixed line naming its step when it looks like a secret, a commit the state records for the batch excepted ([ADR 0088](../../adr/0088-out-doc-text-that-looks-like-a-secret-is-left-out-of-the-pull-request-and-the-status-comment.md)) (`renderStatus`, `src/engine/status-comment.ts`).
  Each edit is a revision in the ledger, `[batchId, "status.<n>"]`, due when the rendered text's digest differs from the latest revision's; it edits the previous revision's comment by its id, and makes the comment again when that was deleted by hand.
  An order is edited at most once a minute, its first comment and the edit to on tap or cancelled at once ([ADR 0072](../../adr/0072-the-status-comment-edits-as-revisions-in-the-ledger.md)).
  A claimed target issue keeps its claim comments and gets no status comment; the Brewery issue that is its order gets the batch's, once the ledger records that issue, and nothing is written before ([ADR 0144](../../adr/0144-a-claimed-target-issue-gets-a-brewery-issue-that-is-its-order.md), [ADR 0155](../../adr/0155-a-claimed-order-s-brewery-issue-gets-the-status-comment-and-the-lane-label.md)).
- A brewed pull request's **body**, the change, which becomes the merge commit's description: written when it opens, and rendered again after each later round's push unless a person or a bot edited it, which the status comment then says under its pull requests ([the openPullRequest action](../recipes/open-pull-request.md), [ADR 0227](../../adr/0227-a-later-round-renders-the-pull-request-s-body-again-unless-someone-edited-it.md)).
- A **merge brief** on a brewed pull request once it is ready to merge: the latest record of the checks on the head the batch pushed finished with none failed, its latest base check of that head found no conflict and no failing check on the merged tree ([ADR 0226](../../adr/0226-a-failing-check-on-the-merged-tree-sends-its-batch-back-to-the-fix-step.md)), and the batch is On tap (`readyOf`, `src/engine/merge-brief.ts`); a head with no checks is ready once its record finished with none, a pass after it was first seen ([ADR 0225](../../adr/0225-a-ready-pull-request-gets-a-merge-brief.md)).
  It is one comment through the ledger per ready record, `[batchId, "brief.<n>"]`, its result the comment and the record it was for (pull request, head, digest of its checks); a record that goes pending and finishes the same posts nothing again, and a re-run, a late check or a fix round's head that is ready posts the next (`mergeBriefEffects`, `src/engine/merge-brief-post.ts`).
  It is found before it is applied by a hidden line naming its record right before its marker, so a kill posts once whatever else changed, and an older record's brief a kill left stays.
  Titled `Merge? <title> @ <short sha>`, the title read as the pull request's issue's and escaped, it says **Ready to merge** and that it waits on whoever merges; what changed since it opened (each fix round, the head that failed and the commit it pushed, or nothing); the evidence on the head (each check with its conclusion and link, or that none ran; how it merges with its base; each other open pull request of its repository its head conflicts with and the files, or that it merges cleanly with the other open pull requests once every one was checked ([ADR 0230](../../adr/0230-open-pull-requests-of-one-repository-are-merge-tree-checked-against-each-other.md)), a pair conflict holding nothing and posting no new brief; the red proof's readings or why it did not run; each check step's checks and the ones the repository does not have; each step not run and why, or skipped by a person; where the last Taster tasted and the fix rounds since; the sensitive paths of the latest push); the findings each route round fixed, dismissed or left as pre-existing, each after the lens of the finding at its place, the open findings of advisory Tasters, and a blocking Taster's step a person's skip left open with each finding its round recorded; and the last Taster's risk left, or none recorded.
  Every model text is on one line under its heading, a text that looks like a secret left out, closing keywords and mentions taken out, and escaped as plain text.
  A brief is outdated once the head of its pull request moved (the batch's latest push that pushed something, or found the branch moved or gone, left it at another commit, or a base check found someone else's push) or a later brief is recorded; while its pull request has no recorded end, it is hidden on GitHub as outdated (`minimizeComment`, classifier `OUTDATED`), one effect through the ledger per brief, `[batchId, "brief.<n>.hidden"]`, due before the next brief's post, found when the comment is already hidden or gone (`outdatedBriefs`, `src/engine/merge-brief.ts`, [ADR 0229](../../adr/0229-a-merge-brief-whose-head-moved-is-hidden-as-outdated.md)).
  A record that goes pending or fails on the same head hides nothing, the brief of a merged or closed pull request stays, and an older record's brief a kill left under the same key is not in the ledger and stays shown.
  The status comment links the latest brief while it is of the record that makes the pull request ready now and not hidden; else, while the pull request is open and no one else pushed to its branch (`openOf`), it says `<url> is not ready to merge yet: no merge brief.` (ADR 0229).
- A **plan comment** on an order's issue once a batch of it has a succeeded plan ([work breakdown](../intake/work-breakdown.md), [ADR 0233](../../adr/0233-a-plan-is-an-out-doc-whose-paths-the-engine-checks-shown-as-one-comment-per-order.md)): a table of the sub-orders, a Mermaid graph of their order with every label quoted, the alternatives when the plan has them, and a review and a gate section that say none yet until the plan lenses and the gate fill them (#587, #588).
  It is one comment per order through the ledger, `[batchId, "plan.<n>"]`, each revision recording the comment and the digest of its text, due only when the latest plan's text changed, with no limit a minute; it is found by its marker, edited in place, made again when deleted by hand, and a later batch of the same order edits the same comment (`planEffects`, `src/engine/plan-comment.ts`).
  Every model text is one escaped line, left out when it looks like a secret.
  It ends with `Full plan:` and a link to the plan's out doc on the Brewery's state branch, `https://<host>/<brewery>/blob/foedrum/state/batches/<batch>/<step>-<attempt>.md?plain=1`, kept when the text is cut, and the status comment ends that step's assumptions with the same link (`outDocLink`, `src/engine/state-branch.ts`, [ADR 0266](../../adr/0266-the-plan-and-status-comments-link-the-plan-s-out-doc-on-the-state-branch.md)).
  `?plain=1` shows the file as written, its JSON frontmatter with every assumption whole; the link names the branch, not a commit, and works from the push after the comment's revision (ADR 0266).
- A **GitHub Projects board** with one column per lane serves as the kanban view until the Brewhouse dashboard (M6).
- **Labels** mirror lanes: a Brewery issue carries one of `foedrum:ordered`, `foedrum:brewing`, `foedrum:conditioning`, `foedrum:stuck` and `foedrum:on-tap` at a time, and none once cancelled (`laneLabelEffects`, `src/engine/lane-labels.ts`).
  Each change is a revision in the ledger, `[batchId, "label.<n>"]`, found when the issue already shows it; a label missing in the Brewery is made before it is added ([ADR 0154](../../adr/0154-lane-labels-on-the-order-s-issue-and-the-status-comment-s-path-line.md)).
  An answer to the Innkeeper's question also labels the Brewery issue `recipe:<name>` with the recipe it chose, once, through the ledger, `[batchId, "recipe-label"]`, found when the issue already shows it and no other, the issue's other `recipe:*` labels taken off (`recipeLabelEffects`, `src/engine/recipe-label.ts`, [ADR 0232](../../adr/0232-foedrum-mash-routes-an-issue-to-the-innkeeper-whose-question-the-answer-chooses-the-recipe-by.md)); it routes the issue's next order, never the batch that asked.
  A claimed target-repository issue carries no lane label, only `foedrum:claimed` until its claim is released ([target-repository issues](../intake/target-issues.md)); the Brewery issue that is its order carries the lane label of the latest batch whose order it is, once the ledger records it ([ADR 0155](../../adr/0155-a-claimed-order-s-brewery-issue-gets-the-status-comment-and-the-lane-label.md)).
- **Commands** in comments, from allow-listed users only, processed exactly once (the comment id is the idempotency key) and acknowledged with a reaction: 👀 received, 🚀 done, 😕 rejected with the reason in the status comment.
