# Foedrum Trademark Policy

This policy is derived from the Model Trademark Guidelines, available at [www.modeltrademarkguidelines.org](http://www.modeltrademarkguidelines.org), which are published under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The changes to them are listed at the end.

## What this policy covers

The Foedrum project's marks are the Foedrum name, its logo and its mascot, whether registered or not.

Foedrum's code is free software under the [GNU Affero General Public License version 3](LICENSE), and so is your copy of it. That licence covers the code only: it gives no right to use the name, the logo or the mascot. This policy says how you may use them.

## The one rule behind all the others

Whenever you use a mark, make it clear what people get and from whom. Never let anyone believe that your product, service or website is the Foedrum project's, or that the project made, checked or endorses it, when it did not.

## What you may do without asking

**Say that your work is for Foedrum or works with it.** Put the Foedrum name after a word that says how your work relates to it, and keep your own name first.
- Allowed: "Hops, a dashboard for Foedrum", "Hops works with Foedrum".
- Not allowed: "Foedrum Hops", "the Foedrum Dashboard".

**Write and speak about Foedrum.** Articles, books, blog posts, talks, videos and tutorials may use the name, including in their titles, as long as they do not suggest that the project wrote or approved them.
- Allowed: a talk titled "Turning issues into pull requests with Foedrum".

**Offer paid services for Foedrum under your own name.** Support, training, consulting and hosting are fine when your own name is what people buy from.
- Allowed: "Malt & Co: Foedrum training and support".
- Not allowed: "Foedrum Training" or "Official Foedrum Hosting" as the name of your service.

**Pass on unchanged copies of Foedrum under its own name.** A copy of an official release, changed in no way, may keep the name and the logo, whether you mirror it, bundle it on a disk or host it.
- Allowed: an internal package mirror that serves the `foedrum` package exactly as it was published.

## What you may not do

**Use the name or logo for a modified Foedrum.** Once you change the code, you are the origin of that version, and people who see the Foedrum name would expect the project's own software. The same goes for a repackaged product that uses Foedrum inside.
- Not allowed: publishing your fork as "Foedrum", "Foedrum Pro" or "Foedrum (patched)", with the Foedrum logo.

**Use a name or logo close enough to be confused with Foedrum's.** This includes a variation, an abbreviation, a translation, a name that sounds the same, and a logo that copies the shape or the mascot.
- Not allowed: "Foedrumm", "Foe-drum", "FoedrumAI", or a mascot that is the Foedrum mascot recoloured.

## Forks

A fork is welcome; the AGPL guarantees it. Give it its own name and its own logo, and you may then say truthfully where it comes from.
- Allowed: "Lagerline, based on Foedrum".
- Not allowed: "Lagerline, the Foedrum fork" as its product name, or Lagerline shipped with the Foedrum logo.

## Names reserved for the project

These names are kept for the Foedrum project alone, and this policy grants no use of them:
- the npm package name `foedrum`;
- npm package names that start with `foedrum-`;
- the npm scope `@foedrum/`.

Choose another name for your package, such as `hops-for-foedrum`.

## Questions and misuse

To ask about a use this policy does not describe, or to report a use that misleads people, open an issue in [Foedrum's repository](https://github.com/EdwinBekaert/foedrum/issues). The maintainer answers there.

## Changes from the Model Trademark Guidelines

- The marks are named: the Foedrum name, logo and mascot; trade dress is not covered.
- The uses allowed without asking are the four above, each with an example; the sections on licensed uses (modified builds, preloaded hardware, user groups, promotional goods), websites, events and combined software are removed.
- The list of marks reserved for the project names the npm package names and scope.
- The sections on trademark notices, on what trademark law is and on writing a mark correctly are removed.
- The text is rewritten in plain words for a person rather than a lawyer.
